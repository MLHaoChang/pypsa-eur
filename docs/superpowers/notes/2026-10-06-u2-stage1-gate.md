**Judgement on each semantic delta:**

- **Year-billed peak pro-rated by hours** (95,000 → 15,356.16 on two months). **Honest.**
  - IC's `capacity` on `peak_import` is EUR/kW-year × represented hours / 8760. GS charged the whole year's price on two months.
  - The two are identical on the 8,760 h a guided study always runs, and `annual_bill` is null off a one-year horizon anyway.
  - The empty `peak_mw_by_billing_period` (`annual_peak_not_in_demand_lines`) is recorded.
  - The LP formulation of the year basis is still unverified. That is WP6's job, as the plan says.

- **Contracted capacity without a stated limit → rated on the PoC size** (None → 14,160). **Defensible, but it hides an assumption: [S2].**
  - GS returned null on purpose (ADR-0001): the contracted MW is unknown.
  - The engine now silently assumes contracted MW = the PoC `p_nom` (= the connection MW in the pack).
  - The bill carries only `capacity_charge_prorated_by_hours`. Nothing says the contracted MW was assumed.
  - No seed has a capacity charge, so the change only affects user tariffs. It is still a null-to-number change with no disclosure. See **C2**.

- **Partial-month fixed charge pro-rated** (200 → 90.55). **Honest.**
  - IC pro-rates by covered hours, and the note `fixed_charge_prorated_on_partial_period` is emitted.
  - It cannot occur on the guided 8,760 h axis.
  - The new codes still need HELP entries plus the frontend mirror before WP8 shows engine bills. The plan already lists this.

- **Metered export without dispatch → null** (`export_line_needs_the_dispatch`). **Honest for the planned callers.**
  - The planned `bill_meter` callers are the preview and the baseline at a perturbed tariff. Both have export 0.
  - It would bite only if WP8 routed an option's bill through `bill_meter`. See **C6**.

- **No export price → compiles unpriced instead of being refused.** **Not honest as built: [S3].**
  - GS refused such a tariff at network build (`write_tariff_prices` → `PackError tariff_export_pricing`). The pack always builds `grid_export`.
  - Compile now accepts it and notes `export_unpriced` ("the export Link earns nothing").
  - But the adapter's `_export_line` returns `None` / `no_export_price` as soon as any export flows. I probed this in memory with 1 kWh exported in one hour: `export_credit=None` and `total=None`.
  - Once WP6 removes link prices, an unpriced export Link costs nothing. The LP can then route PV surplus to it on a degenerate tie with curtailment, so a PV option's whole bill and case become "not established" depending on the solver.
  - Compile and adapter contradict each other, and this would ship in the U2 PR. The recorded cause (`export_priced_only_through_a_series`) does not mention the consequence. See **C1**.

- Two deltas are bookkeeping and correctly recorded: the `Bill.engine` literal and the seventh component.

**[S5] The export-series delete workaround (`compile.delete_export_series`).**
- *What is safe:*

  - (c) The workaround reaches IC internals outside the frozen facade (`db.models.LibraryItem`, `bundle_pins.read_pins`, `storage_paths.library_file`) and calls `db.commit()` on the caller's session.
  - (d) **No test covers org isolation.** My mutation M6 removed the `org_id` filter, and all 37 compile tests stayed green.
- The name, idempotence and versioning are tested: same price gives v1 again, a changed price gives v2, a copied study's series survives the origin's delete, and a pinned series is kept.
- The owner's label lives only in `description`. `SeriesMeta.label` stays the helper's "Flat export price 40 EUR/MWh", so the Library list does not name the study yet. This is an engine ask, recorded.
