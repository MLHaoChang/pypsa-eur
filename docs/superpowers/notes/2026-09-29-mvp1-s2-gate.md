## (1) Library seed against the v0.14.0 extraction and the plan

- **[OK] Values, units, currency year and sources.** I checked every row against `docs/superpowers/notes/2026-09-28-mvp1-s2-seed-rows-technology-data-v0.14.0.csv`. The 12 sourced rows match exactly:
  - battery inverter: 213.9279 EUR/kW, FOM 0.3375 %/yr, efficiency 0.96, lifetime 10;
  - battery storage: 189.861 EUR/kWh, lifetime 25;
  - solar-utility: 482.4785 EUR/kW_e, FOM 2.4757 %/yr, lifetime 40;
  - solar-rooftop: 883.8138 EUR/kW_e, FOM 1.4234 %/yr, lifetime 40.

  Every row has currency year 2020, and the source strings are verbatim. `test_the_technology_rows_are_the_v0_14_0_transcription` pins this against the note file.
- **[OK] Source year and URL.** `source_year=2026` is the v0.14.0 release date in `data/versions.csv` (2026-02-13). `source_url` is the tagged raw `costs_2030.csv`.
- **[OK] Ranges.** Every range is ±30 % marked `assumed`, and efficiencies are clipped at 1.0; both are documented and tested. The discount-rate range is 0.049 to 0.091, assumed. This follows the S1 nit: `LedgerRange.source` stays `assumed | source`.
- **[OK] Derived round-trip efficiency.** The RTE row is 0.9216 = 0.96². The loader refuses a derived row that disagrees with its formula. `refresh_derived` makes a default derived row follow an edited input and leaves a row the user set alone. This is tested.
- **[OK] Not-used rows.** The two degradation rows have value `null` and `unavailable={value: not_used_in_mvp1}`. They carry no range and no flag, and are not key drivers (ADR-0001 null plus a flag).
- **[S1] The seed costs are 2030 projections, and nothing says so** (BC-S2-5). The plan pins `outputs/costs_2030.csv`, so 189.9 EUR/kWh and 213.9 EUR/kW are technology-data's costs projected for 2030, in 2020 EUR. The ledger shows them to a site owner deciding in 2026 as "Danish Energy Agency, 2026". Only the URL's filename gives the projection year away.
  - Separately, and not binding, the owner may want to reconsider `costs_2025`. That is a plan-level choice.
- **[N1] The derived row loses its formula.** It shows `source="derived"` in the ledger, CSV and report. The formula lives only in the CSV `note` column, which is not carried to the `LedgerRow`. Put "derived: battery_inverter_efficiency squared" in `source`.
- **[N2] The derived row's range does not follow its input.** The RTE range (0.6451 to 1.0) is ±30 % of 0.9216. Squaring the inverter-efficiency range would give 0.4516 to 1.0.
- **[N3] The rooftop PV source string is uninformative.** "Calculated. See 'further description'." is a verbatim transcription. Say "technology-data v0.14.0 calculation (DEA-based)".
- **[N4] The discount-rate source overstates its role.** It describes 0.07 as "the default rate PyPSA-Eur annualises technology-data costs with". It is `costs.fill_values`, a fill-in for technologies without their own rate. I checked that `config/config.default.yaml` has `discount rate: 0.07` there and that `CITATION.cff` is v2026.02.0.

## (2) `seed_ledger` and `apply_user_row` against decision 11

- **[OK] Seeding.** Every seeded row is `provenance=library` and `status=default`, and the flags are exactly the key drivers (mutation-proven).
- **[OK] Units and edits.**
  - The unit is required and must match the row exactly. It is refused, never converted; surrounding whitespace is stripped, case is not folded.
  - Edits are pure, and a non-finite value is refused.
  - The route refuses `imported` provenance.
  - The citation becomes "User entry" in the edit year unless the user gives one.
- **[OK] Re-seed.** Library rows are refreshed, user rows are kept, and a user row whose key the library no longer carries is kept with an honesty note.
- **[S2] Edits to rows nothing uses are accepted and stored as `customised`** (BC-S2-2). Probed:
  - `battery_storage_degradation_calendar_pct_per_year := 2.0` is accepted, even though MVP-1 models no degradation.
  - On the time-of-use tariff, `demand_charge_price := 5000` is accepted. The tariff has no demand charge, so S3/S4 will either ignore the number silently or invent a demand charge the tariff does not have.
  - A re-seed after switching to the time-of-use tariff keeps a user demand charge of 8000 as `customised`. It adds no note and no `needs_attention`, and the unit is not checked against the fresh row.

  This is the class of defect that S1's `_refuse_outside_mvp1` already refuses: "not stored and silently ignored".
- **[S3] Physically impossible values are accepted and count towards feasibility** (BC-S2-3). Probed, all accepted as `customised`:
  - `battery_inverter_efficiency = 1.5` (derived RTE becomes **2.25**). The "96 typed as per unit" case is the same failure.
  - `battery_storage_eur_per_kwh = -100`.
  - `discount_rate = -0.5`.
  - `battery_storage_lifetime_years = 0`, which gives `horizon_and_replacements` a result of `(0, ())`.
  - `energy_price_level = 0`.

  A unit string that matches does not make the value's scale right. An RTE above 1 is free energy in the S4 LP.
- **[S4] The currency year of a user money value is silently mixed or silently assumed** (BC-S2-4).
  - `apply_user_row(..., currency_year=2026)` on the battery cost is accepted with no note. The study's other rows are in 2020 EUR, so the S5 pro forma would mix years. Spec decision 7 says "Mixing bases across screens is a defect".
  - When the currency year is not given, a user's 2026 quote is tagged 2020 without comment.
  - `DecisionStudy.currency_year` can also be PATCHed to a year other than the library's, and the ledger does not notice.
- **[S5] A user value outside its range keeps the library's range** (carry to S6). A value of 400 EUR/kWh keeps the range 132.9 to 246.8. S6's tornado evaluates at `range.low` and `range.high`, and decision 6's `marginal` reads the sign flip inside those ranges. A range that does not bracket the base value makes both wrong. S6 must pin one of these:
  - re-centre an assumed range on the user's value;
  - take a user range;
  - use the hull of the range and the value.

  Nothing sets `needs_attention` today either.
- **[N5] "Confirming" a library value replaces its citation.** Re-entering a library value makes it "User entry, 2026", so the DEA citation disappears for an unchanged number. Consider keeping the library citation when the value is equal.
- **[N6] The model comment contradicts S2's practice.** The comment on `models/study.py::LedgerRow.currency_year` says None for non-money rows. S2 sets 2020 on lifetimes, efficiencies and the multiplier. The plan's acceptance needs it on the `discount_rate` and `energy_price_level` key rows. Update the comment, and have BC-S2-4 find money rows by unit, not by `currency_year` presence.

## (3) `maturity_from_ledger` against decision 12

- **[OK] The rules follow the plan.**
  - `screening` holds while any key driver is default or `needs_attention`, or while the load is synthetic or missing.
  - `feasibility` needs every key driver customised or measured and an uploaded load.
  - `design` is reserved.
  - Reasons name each row as `"<key>: <status> (<provenance>)"`, plus `"load: …"`.
  - With no key drivers the study never reaches feasibility.
  - PATCH recomputes when a ledger is stored.
- **[OK] AACE bands.** Both ends of each AACE 18R-97 range are now stored: Class 5 is -20/-50 and +30/+100, Class 4 is -15/-30 and +20/+50. Both match the RP. The `reference` says "indicative analogy", so no estimate class is claimed.
- **[S6] Feasibility is reachable on an illustrative tariff** (BC-S2-1). I re-entered the five (or, on the time-of-use tariff, four) key-driver values unchanged with an uploaded load. The result was `feasibility` with reasons `[]` on both seed tariffs. Both tariffs say "Illustrative figures … replace them with the site's own bill", and in S2 an intake can only choose a library tariff (`_intake_tariff` refuses anything else). So every `feasibility` badge MVP-1 can produce today is on an illustrative tariff that prices every hour. Decision 12 names generic versus quoted inputs as a maturity factor.
- **[S7] The `not_applicable` skip is untested.** Removing it turns nothing red. Add a test when BC-S2-1 lands, using a non-library tariff injected in the test.
- **[N7] Two maturity answers before the first PUT.** GET `/ledger` answers `screening`, while GET `/{study_id}` still says `not_established`. This is acceptable, but S8 should read one of them.
- **[N8] `test_measured_rows_count_as_established` does not test measured provenance.** It also sets `status=customised`, so it would pass without the `provenance == "measured"` clause.

## (4) Routes

- **[OK] 404s.** Both an unknown study and a non-member get 404, never 403, and the sidecar is byte-unchanged.

## (6) The four readings

- **"Flat time-of-use reference" as a two-band time-of-use tariff with no demand charge: acceptable.**
  - The bands partition the week, and this is tested hour by hour, so the first-match rule S3 pinned and a partition reading agree.
  - The German seed already provides the flat energy price.
  - Export is below import in every hour on both seeds, which is S4's acceptance, and this is tested.
  - Pin the reading in the plan text: "one flat price per band set, no demand charge".
- **Network-charge basis `per_mwh`: acceptable.** It matches S3's committed `_NETWORK_CHARGE_BASES = (per_mwh, per_period, per_year)`. `models/study.py::NetworkCharge.basis` is still a free `str`, which is S3's to tighten.
- **The monthly-peak "German-style" tariff: acceptable with its note.** The honesty note is correct: German DSOs bill the annual peak (Jahresleistungspreis). But the note lives only in `Tariff.honesty_notes`. The ledger payload does not carry it, so S5, S7 and S8 must show it wherever the tariff's name appears. S3 already refuses `annual_peak`.
- **PV rows seeded regardless of intake: acceptable.** They are not key drivers, so maturity is unaffected. S4 must choose rooftop or utility by intake. S7 must mark PV rows as not feeding any option when PV is off, or the assumptions chapter lists inputs that no figure used.

## (7) Scope, behaviour, style, and what later phases will trip over

- **[OK] Scope.**
  - The changes are additive. `AccuracyBand` gains three optional fields and `DecisionStudy` gains `ledger`; the S1 round trips are unchanged, still 88.
  - PATCH behaviour changes only when a ledger is stored.
  - No economic surface is touched and no existing number moves.
  - The library is vendored and never downloaded, and an unknown version is refused.
  - `study_library/` is missing from `pypsa-gui.spec`. That is S9's job and is disclosed.
- **[S8] `energy_price_level` has three meanings** (carry to S4/S6, and pin before S4). The label says "multiplier on the tariff's bands". The `technical_name` says "links_t.marginal_cost scale on grid_import", which since S3 includes the per-MWh network charge. Plan S6 says the energy-price row "scales the time-of-use bands around their time-weighted mean", which changes the spread, not the level, and is a no-op on the flat German seed. Pick one, and write it into the plan, the row's label and `technical_name`, and one S4 helper, for example `tariff_from_ledger(tariff, ledger)`.
- **[S9] The tariff price is held twice** (carry to S4). The demand-charge price exists both in the `Tariff` object and in the `demand_charge_price` ledger row. The pack, the bill calculator and the tornado must take the ledger value (the key driver) and the tariff's basis. A PATCH that changes `intake.tariff` leaves the stored ledger's tariff rows stale until a `reseed`, and nothing flags this. S4 should reseed or record the source tariff id on the rows.
- **[S10] Two rows claim the battery efficiency fields** (carry to S4). `battery_inverter_efficiency` (technical name "efficiency_store, efficiency_dispatch") and `battery_round_trip_efficiency` both describe them. Plan S4 uses `sqrt(rte)`, so the RTE row must be the one the pack reads. If a user sets the RTE directly, the inverter row stays at 0.96. Fix the inverter row's `technical_name`, for example "input to battery_round_trip_efficiency".
- **[OK] Two-lifetimes inputs are present as ledger rows.** `battery_inverter_lifetime_years` (10) and `battery_storage_lifetime_years` (25) are there. `library.horizon_and_replacements` gives `(25, (10, 20))` and follows user edits; this is tested. `finance_defaults.yaml` names the rule by ledger key.
- **Nits on the lifetime rule:**
  - Python's `round` turns 12.5 into 12. Pin floor, ceil or integer-only lifetimes in S5.
  - technology-data has no FOM row for battery storage, so S4's "(plus FOM)" applies to the inverter investment only; say so.
- **Also for S4:**
  - Replace `library.BESS_KEY_DRIVERS` with `questions.BESS_AT_SITE.key_drivers`, with a test that they are equal.
  - BC-2's `p_nom_max` multiple needs a ledger row. `_TECH_KEYS` refuses unknown library rows, so add it through the finance or pack path.
  - The default tariff when none is chosen is the file's first one, the German seed. Add an honesty note such as `tariff_not_chosen_library_default`.
- **[N13] The tariff loader ignores the CSV `unit` column.** A future edit to "EUR/kW/month" would be read as EUR/MW. Assert the unit per component, so the vendored file cannot be silently misread.
- **[N14] `AccuracyBand` does not check its ends.** Nothing validates `low_pct <= low_pct_narrow` and `high_pct_narrow <= high_pct`.
- **House style.** The code cites function names, the docstrings state the rules, the tests red-first are recorded, and ruff is clean in the tree.

## Binding conditions (satisfy in S2, re-verify, then proceed)

**BC-S2-1. An illustrative tariff holds the badge at screening.** `maturity_from_ledger` (or its route caller) keeps `screening` while the study's tariff is a library seed (`source == "illustrative"`), with a reason naming it, for example `tariff: de_industrial_illustrative (illustrative, library)`.
- Test: the all-customised plus uploaded case stays `screening` on each seed tariff, and reaches `feasibility` on a non-library tariff injected in the test. The same test covers the `not_applicable` skip ([S7]).
- Show it red first.

**BC-S2-2. Edits to unused or inapplicable rows are refused, not stored.**
- `apply_user_row`, and so PUT, refuses with a 422 naming the row and the reason when:
  - the row is marked `not_used_in_mvp1`; or
  - the row is `not_applicable` under the chosen tariff.
- On `reseed_ledger`, a kept user row is marked `needs_attention` and gets an honesty note naming it when:
  - its fresh seed is `not_applicable`; or
  - its fresh seed's unit differs from the kept row's.

  It is never silently kept as `customised`.
- Tests for the three cases.

**BC-S2-3. Values outside a row's physical domain are refused.** `apply_user_row` refuses them with the row and the bound named. At minimum:
- efficiencies and RTE in (0, 1];
- lifetimes of at least 1 year;
- costs, prices and charges of at least 0;
- the discount rate in a stated real-terms interval, for example [0, 0.5);
- `energy_price_level` greater than 0.

Tests include `battery_inverter_efficiency = 96` and a storage lifetime of 0.

**BC-S2-4. One currency year, never mixed silently.**
- A money-row edit (the unit contains the tariff or study currency) whose `currency_year` differs from the study's is refused with "enter the value in <year> EUR". The study's year is `DecisionStudy.currency_year` when set, else the library's.
- There is no conversion.
- A study `currency_year` that differs from the ledger's is named in the ledger's `honesty_notes` or refused at PATCH.
- Test both.

**BC-S2-5. The 2030 projection is labelled.** The technology cost rows state that they are technology-data's **2030 projections**, for example `source_year` note or label text "(2030 projection)", and a ledger honesty note `technology_costs_are_2030_projections_in_2020_eur`. A test asserts the note, so a 2026 reader of the ledger, CSV or report is not told these are current prices.
