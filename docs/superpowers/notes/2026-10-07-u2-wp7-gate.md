### 2. Bounds (claim 5) and meter Links (claim 6)

**[S2] The CAPEX bound is a WORKAROUND against the parent contract, and the parent plan does not record it.**
- *Contract:* parent §4 C5 (amended at the IC S0b gate) says "CAPEX bounds go **only** through `finance.case.scale_capex(case, f)`", and that `dataclasses.replace(asset, overnight_cost=…)` on an asset with parts raises.

- *Justification:* it is needed. `scale_capex` is uniform, and a ledger row prices one part of one asset.
- *Risk:* it depends on IC internals outside the frozen facade (`AssetFinance.parts` / `AssetPart` fields, `TemplateLine.key` / `source` / `stream`).
- *Record:* the coordinator's approval (2026-10-07) is recorded only in GS's sub-plan status block. The parent plan and the IC S0b findings note still state the "only through `scale_capex`" rule.
- See **X6**.

- G2 keeps the asset rates at the centre. It turns both rate-parity tests red, so the asset-rate leg is load-bearing and pinned.

**[N6] The `BY_CONSTRUCTION` codes sit on price-bound views too.**
- *Mechanism:* `option_case` always calls `_view(..., centre=True)`, and a price bound IS an `option_case` on the variant network. Its view therefore says `npv_nonnegative_at_optimum_by_construction` even when its NPV is −61,423 EUR (the driver's `demand_charge_price` low bound).
- *Impact today:* none. A price bound's bundle is not kept, and only its NPV is read (`_battery_npv_at`).
- The claim "the codes appear at the centre only" is true for what users see, not for the object. Pass `centre=False` from `_price_bound`, e.g. an `option_case(..., centre=...)` keyword.
