PyPSA Studio · design proposal

Studio UX Overhaul

2026-10-14Baseline master b2f6a5a, M3 PR #245 at 4fab3bdStatus: waiting on owner decisions D1–D4

I walked every surface of the running app in Expert and Guided on the Data Center Energy Hub template, before and after solving. This page shows what I found, a proposed structure for features and results, design rules, and mockups. Each finding is labelled M measured (a screenshot, a source grep or a contrast calculation) or O opinion.

In short

Five problems, one fix each

  • MResults don't lead with key results. 14 flat tabs, only 7 visible at 1440 px, and the panel opens on Capacity Expansion. Fix: Summary first (M3), then four groups: Investment, Grid plan, Reliability, Operation.
  • MResults that haven't run read as zero. Transmission losses show "0 MWh" when losses were not modelled, and the Guided Results step is just greyed out. Fix: one result-state component with six states, and no number unless one was computed.
  • MMetrics are inconsistent. There are seven KPI tile implementations, the same cost appears as €39.75 M, €39.7m and 31.5M, and dates come in three formats. Fix: one Kpi component and one format.ts.
  • MThe workspace is crowded. With a panel open, the canvas shrinks to about 220 px. The assistant dock keeps 420 px even when it can't answer. Fix: at most three columns at once, and the dock collapses to a rail.
  • MCharts are hard to read. Axis titles overlap the ticks, some ticks have no units, chart titles are cut off, and no targets or limits are drawn. Fix: one ChartCard standard with reference lines and highlights.
For the owner

Decisions D1–D4

These go through the coordinator as interactive choices. Nothing in a section is built until you've picked its design.

D1 · Results grouping

  1. Two levels: Summary · Investment · Grid plan · Reliability · Operation recommended
    Five top tabs, and none of them overflow. Existing tab ids keep working for the assistant and deep links. Layout tests are updated deliberately.
  2. Keep one flat strip, reordered, with group dividers
    The smallest change. The strip still overflows at 1440 px with the dock open.
  3. Groups only inside Summary
    Summary is grouped, but the detail tabs stay as they are today.

D2 · Where Emissions and Prices live

  1. Emissions under Operation, Prices under Investment recommended
    Each sits with the question it answers: emissions come from dispatch, and prices drive revenue. This keeps four groups.
  2. A fifth group, "Markets & environment"
    Gives these two more prominence, at the cost of one more tab and a group that guided chapters don't use.

D3 · Metrics that weren't modelled

  1. A "Not modelled" pill, no number, and the setting that turns it on recommended
    Meets the "never shown as zero" rule. It's a display change only, but the agreed list of which metrics count as not modelled comes back for your review.
  2. Keep showing 0, with a "not modelled" caption (as Load Flow does today)
    No new component, but a reader can still copy the 0 into a report.

D4 · Which slices start first

  1. S1 KPI and format standard + S2 result states, now; S3 grouping after M3 merges recommended
    The lowest risk and the most visible gain, with no collision with M3's Results.tsx work.
  2. S3 grouping first
    The structure is visible sooner, but it has to wait for M3 and rebase onto it.
  3. S5 workspace density first
    Fixes the crowding first. It needs the companion stream's agreement on the AssistantDock change.

Proposal

One vocabulary for tools, results, Guided chapters and reports

Each category answers one question. The same four names appear in the Expert sidebar, in the Results groups, in the Guided steps and in the report chapters. Project-wide things (scenarios, snapshots, reports, the queue) stay outside them.

CategoryQuestionInputs and toolsResults (today's tabs)
InvestmentWhat do we build, and what does it cost and earn?Assets, capacity bounds, Economics (rates, costs, tariffs, finance), Decision studyCapacity Expansion, Economics, Prices, Investment
Grid planCan the network and the connection carry it?Network, Campus electrical, Grid codesLoad Flow, grid-code compliance, short circuit
ReliabilityDoes the critical load stay on?ENS target, reserve margin, outage data, stress scenariosAdequacy, Lost load, FMEA
OperationHow does it run hour by hour?Horizon, time series, dispatch and solver settings, island operationDispatch, Storage cycling, Curtailment, Emissions
Project—Scenarios, Snapshots, Reports, Solve queue, IssuesCompare (rail), Asset detail (drawer)

The group layer is presentation only. ResultsTab ids stay the same, so the assistant's navigation and deep links keep working. If a group becomes a navigable target, the assistant inventory is regenerated with its tool, never by hand.

Design rules

Units, spacing, type

QuantityKPITable

Time2030-07-01 14:00ISO, 24 h, everywhere. Axis ticks Jul 01, with the year in the subtitle
  • typeFive sizes (11, 12, 13, 16, 22 px) replace ten arbitrary ones. All spacing is on a 4 px grid.
  • colourPrimary buttons use accent-700 (4.5 : 1). Axis and secondary text go up to at least 4.5 : 1. Carrier colours come only from colourForCarrier. Good, warning and bad are never shown in the accent colour.
  • copyGuided shows display names, never ids. No developer paths. Method prose goes behind "How this is computed".
