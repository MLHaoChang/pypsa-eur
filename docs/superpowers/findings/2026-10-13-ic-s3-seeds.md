# IC S3 seeds: templates and Energy Hub placeholders from the generic defaults pack

**Date:** 2026-10-13. **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-13-ic-engine-asks.md` §7. **Owner decisions
(2026-10-13):** Q1 a (extend the pack, derive everything), Q2 a (accept the pack values, re-pin, record before/after),
the spare conversion priced as a fuel cell, the spare storage as the pack's 4 h battery without the trains factor.

## What landed

- **Pack 2026-10-13** = 2026-10-07 byte for byte plus 19 rows from technology-data v0.14.0 (`costs_2030.csv`, 2020 EUR):
  `gas-genset` ← OCGT, `diesel-genset` ← oil (DEA diesel engine farm), `onwind`, `fuel-cell` (LT-PEMFC), `electrolysis`
  (AEC; investment from the IEA e-fuels report); one `investment` part per MW: overnight, fom_share, lifetime and
  (except onwind) efficiency, ranges assumed ±30 % (efficiency capped at 1). Hash pinned.
- **`defaults_pack.pricing.price_from_pack(n, component, name, technology, *, version)`**, the one way to price an asset
  from the pack: a battery through `derive.apply_parts` (two parts, `capital_cost` derived); any other asset one
  investment part on PyPSA's `overnight_cost` / `lifetime` / `fom_cost` at the pack's discount rate (7 %); a Link per MW
  of input (× efficiency). Each typed field it writes is stamped `default` through `asset_schema.provenance.record`
  (source `generic_defaults@2026-10-13#<hash16>`, source year, money year, range, illustrative); derived fields
  (`capital_cost`, a battery's `lifetime` / `fom_cost`) are never recorded.
- **Templates** (`project_templates/eh_templates.py`, `TEMPLATE_PACK_VERSION`): all 8 extendable candidates priced from
  the pack (eh_datacenter genset_new, bess_new; eh_h2_hub wind_new, solar_new, fuel_cell, bess_new; eh_microgrid
  diesel_new, bess_new). Fuel and grid marginal prices are not in the pack and are unchanged.
- **Redundancy scenarios** (`services/adequacy/redundancy.py`): the spare generator a gas genset, the spare storage the
  pack's 4 h battery (its cost per MW no longer × trains; the trains scale its size), the invented spare conversion a
  fuel cell; `cost_basis` `defaults_pack:2026-10-13`; each applied record carries a `pricing` block.

## Who reads "latest"

No production code resolves the pack's latest version: the guided study pins 2026-10-05 (`study.library.PACK_VERSION`,
its bump tripwire passes), the templates and redundancy pin 2026-10-13, and the campus route reads
`campus_assets.yaml`, not the pack. Only tests call `load_defaults_pack()` without a version. The 2026-10-07 rows are
byte-identical in 2026-10-13, so a reader of either sees the same campus numbers.
