Note the layering: the OEDI record states CC BY 4.0. The ATB disclaimer page itself does not mention licence, copyright or redistribution. It is not public domain per OEDI (CC BY, not CC0/PD). The nlr.gov notice also says use of documents "may be subject to U.S. and foreign Copyright Laws". The indemnification clause ("USER AGREES TO INDEMNIFY DOE/NREL/ALLIANCE...") is a contractual burden the owner should review.

## Recommendation: common (with attribution and disclaimer carried along; owner to review indemnity clause)
