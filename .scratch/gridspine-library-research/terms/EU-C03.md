## Recommendation: common
**open_verified confirmed** for data "published on Energi Data Service": the terms apply CC BY 4.0 to all data on the platform with no stated third-party carve-out or exclusion on the terms page. Residual caveats: (1) terms say nothing of upstream rights in individual datasets (e.g. data originating from third parties such as exchanges or neighbouring TSOs); dataset pages were not individually checked; (2) availability is not guaranteed; (3) API rate limits are not on this page (not read); (4) the catalog's "no key" claim is not confirmed by the terms page.
