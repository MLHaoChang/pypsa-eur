## Recommendation: common (for Market data visuals / Download-section market data only)
**open_verified confirmed, with scope limit.** The CC BY 4.0 grant is explicitly limited to data from the "Market data visuals" section and the larger time series in the "Download" section. Other website material (articles, text, graphics) is only covered by the general "freely available for public use" statement under section 111d EnWG, not by CC BY 4.0. No third-party carve-out is listed on the page, but the page states SMARD gets the data from ENTSO-E; ENTSO-E's own terms were not read, so upstream rights are not independently verified. Catalog wording "all exchange data" should not be assumed.
