# Parameter-to-source coverage and explicit gaps

Status: family-level research mapping complete. Exact selected source cells, supported native field binding, data/rights validation and actual imports are implementation gates. A linked source does not populate every field in a family.

All80 inventoried families have a disposition. Europe and North America are the only target regions; geography remains source-specific. Most technical/failure data is not universally applicable. Restricted/unknown references cannot become common-library values. A source link does not inherit the family-level coverage class: the structured join records its narrower role (for example weather context, generic model structure or synthetic fixture).
