# Technical component and control-reference sources

Research date: 2026-10-20 (Europe/Berlin). Scope: Europe and North America. This is source research, not installed integration, bulk extraction, legal approval, equipment certification or engineering validation. No accounts were created, fees paid or data owners contacted. `technical-sources.json` is the machine-readable catalogue of the 18 source families below.

Each source now includes `mapped_family_ids` tied to the actual80-family [parameter inventory](PARAMETER-INVENTORY.md). Mapping is deliberately partial: for example, a motor-efficiency catalogue can support part of G13, but supplies neither its starting-current curve nor a validated acceleration model. `mapping_scope` records these limits.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Reuse native PyPSA/pandapower types and GridSpine's asset/control/default packs first.** Their existing technical parameters should become version-pinned equipment/library entries rather than another independently maintained table.
2. **Add actual product/performance catalogues selectively:** ENERGY STAR UPS and selected equipment; CEC PV/inverter/storage lists after checking the exact data's terms; EU EPREL heating/cooling products after accepting the applicable public-API terms. Add wind curves from the NLR archive with per-model source tracing.
3. **Use SimBench and SMART-DS as synthetic/reference fixtures**, clearly separated from equipment quotes, actual site inventories and validated project models.
4. **Keep detailed certification/model directories behind a rights gate.** AHRI expressly requires a subscription licence for embedding directory data in software or databases. Eurovent/KEYMARK public search/API availability does not itself grant redistribution. Code/regulatory documents require jurisdiction/version/applicability and their own reuse review.

Priorities below are product/research priorities, not permission to redistribute. `open_verified` means a relevant open licence was identified, with its obligations; it does not establish that every linked third-party file has the same rights. `public_terms_review`/`mixed` records must not enter a public/common seed pack until the precise extract and intended use are cleared.

## Model integration rules

The source catalogue maps into existing/proposed `equipment`, `control_preset`, `profile_generator` and source-backed requirement/library records. It should not introduce a second database/store. A technical item needs manufacturer/model/revision or archetype identity, region,50/60 Hz, rated voltage/power/energy, phase/wiring, units and per-unit base, reference temperatures/installation, test standard and measurement/fitting status. Applicable optional fields include positive/zero-sequence impedance, transformer vector group/taps/losses, fault-current envelope, power-factor/reactive envelope, thermal/part-load curves, SOC/temperature operating envelope, min up/down/ramp/restart and controller/plant model compatibility.

- A line/transformer item holds either the native named standard type or explicit parameters, according to existing library rules; don't supply both and allow the engine to overwrite values unnoticed.
- Convert hp/kW, kVA/kW, DC/AC ratings, line-line/phase voltage and impedance bases explicitly. Treat50/60 Hz compatibility as applicability, not an automatic rescaling of every field.
- Preserve test conditions. SEER/SCOP is not instantaneous COP; a rated motor mechanical output is not electrical input; a listed ESS energy is not aged usable energy or firm MW.
- Product identity/efficiency listing does not establish outage rates, warranties, protection coordination, grid-code compliance, GFM behavior or suitability for the exact customer plant. Unknown fields stay unknown.
- Dynamic model files/FMU binaries are executable artifacts, not ordinary parameter rows. Require model/version/licence admission, isolation and independent numerical validation. Generic model defaults remain illustrative until calibrated.
- The five cards reference the same version-pinned data: Energy uses efficiencies/limits, Grid uses electrical parameters, Reliability uses admitted outage data (researched separately), Commercial uses separate cost rows and Control uses admitted plant/preset parameters. Removing an analysis does not delete shared equipment properties.

## Coverage gaps and practical seed

There is no verified universal open equipment catalogue covering switchgear fault duty, industrial motors' starting curves, DC UPS transient behavior, all BESS cells/packs, electrolyser/Haber-Bosch dynamic maps and site-specific OEM controls. Manufacturer datasheets and customer documents should be importable to a private/local library with source spans and rights metadata; do not fabricate missing values or promote them automatically to a common pack. The cost/lifecycle agent is separately assessing DEA/technology-data/ATB/process references; those can supply class-level process assumptions with their applicability recorded.

For the first seed, I recommend native types with frequency gating; a small reviewed PV/inverter/UPS subset; a few traceable turbine curves; and one EU plus one US reference-grid fixture. Hold EPREL/thermal directory bulk ingestion behind exact API/terms checks. Canada needs a portal-access/export feasibility check; **no verified Mexico-specific open technical product catalogue was established here**. Do not label US/Canadian catalogues North-America-wide approval. Keep GB, EU/EEA and national connection requirements separate.

## Validation before promotion

Use schema/unit and domain bounds; native type round-trips; snapshot/source hashes; exact per-model rows and certificate status;50/60 Hz refusal; transformer impedance conversion; coefficient provenance and limiting/part-load checks; duplicate/model-alias handling; discontinued/revoked records; import/update diff preserving local overrides; terms/attribution manifests. For benchmark grids compare a small feeder's expected load-flow result with its original engine/model boundary. Neither a successful import nor a certification listing supplies customer/domain approval.

Access failures were not bypassed: some OEDI/SimBench/NRCan direct opens timed out, rate-limited or returned403, while official catalogue/search/docs supplied the observations above. No bulk-download or live adapter-readiness claim is made.
