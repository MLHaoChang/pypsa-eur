# Cost, operating expense, lifetime and degradation source research

Research date: 2026-10-20 (Europe/Berlin). Scope: Europe and North America (US, Canada and Mexico), across GridSpine's energy, grid, reliability, commercial and control cards. This is source research and a proposed data integration plan: no packages, models or importers were executed, no paid data acquired and no source owners contacted. Machine-readable source register: [cost-sources.json](cost-sources.json), 18 records.

## Recommended library spine

Use the existing versioned `defaults_pack`/PyPSA technology-data provenance as the European starting point. It already supplies cost, lifetime and efficiency rows; add reviewed data sets and regional variants rather than a second cost database. North American records need regional provenance, not merely USD/CAD/MXN conversion. Preserve project pins and allow explicit local/vendor overrides.

The strongest next data adapters are **US ATB**, **Canadian CER Energy Future**, and selected **Danish Energy Agency** workbook revisions. **PNNL storage** is particularly useful for power/energy cost structure, augmentation and replacement assumptions. **UK DESNZ** provides a separate UK assumption set. DOE H2A/NETL and selected battery studies expand process and lifecycle detail after the cost basis/rights mapping is settled.

“Public” and “openly redistributable” are separate facts. `open_verified` below means a source-level reuse grant was found, with its exceptions; it does not grant model validity or override third-party rights. For several useful sources, public access is established but common-library redistribution remains a terms-review gate.

## Regional interpretation

- **Europe:** DEA/technology-data is a strong representative planning backbone, not a tariff or every-country vendor-price database. Keep UK DESNZ as a distinct regional set. A German-specific cross-check is the public [Fraunhofer ISE2024 LCOE study](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/de/veroeffentlichungen/studien/studie-stromgestehungskosten-erneuerbare-energien.html); its published assumptions can be reviewed, but no blanket redistribution permission or automated cost feed is established here. The [JRC CETO battery report](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC145289) is useful context, not a discovered normalized cost/lifecycle feed.
- **United States:** ATB and PNNL offer better structured starting points than scraping miscellaneous vendor brochures. EIA actual-cost aggregates and reference engineering estimates serve different checks. PVSCM offers detailed component boundary checks.
- **Canada:** CER supplies a genuinely region-adjusted dataset. The methodology uses Canadian labour/import/province factors and reference-year FX/inflation. Store its CAD money basis and provincial applicability rather than reconverting US values independently and losing those adjustments.
- **Mexico:** planning documents and COPAR references are credible leads; structured cost availability and rights remain gaps. Preserve a user-confirmed/imported local set or explicitly assumed adapted comparator; never give it verified-Mexico status.

## Library-worthy parameter groups

1. Overnight/component/installed-system investment; EPC/owner/contingency/connection scope; capacity and process throughput denominators; AC/DC nameplate basis.
2. Fixed O&M and variable O&M separately; scheduled maintenance costs, staffing/insurance/tax/land already included in a benchmark; fuel and consumables excluded/included.
3. Construction duration and lead-time scope, economic/technical design life, refurbishment/augmentation/replacement components, decommissioning cost and salvage assumptions.
4. Efficiency/heat rate, auxiliary/parasitic energy, part-load bounds, capacity factor/resource class and physical losses; manufacturer/site models may override generic planning values.
5. Battery chemistry-specific empirical ageing metadata, SOC/temperature/C-rate/DoD/duty cycle, tested EOL threshold, calendar/cycle exposure and replacement resets; no universal annual fade scalar.
6. Process conversion/yield and feedstock/cooling/water boundaries, equipment scale and validity ranges, ramp/minimum-load/start assumptions when data supports them.

Site quotes, actual asset age/health, plant maintenance history, warranty terms and customer replacement policy belong to scoped project/org records. A generalized public assumption can suggest a starting value but cannot turn these site facts into a common-library truth. Financing, inflation and FX series are covered by other research streams; cost rows must reference their conversion basis, not bake them into an undocumented number.

## Integration and validation requirements

Extend the native library/default-pack machinery with versioned technology-assumption sets or approved kinds; do not replace `items.py`. Each atomic row needs source/release/table-cell locator and hash, geography, technology/configuration/size/vintage, observation versus scenario estimate, value/unit, currency and money year, cost scope, scenario/model year, engineering/financial lifetime definition, validity bounds and rights decision. Store raw value and normalized value, conversion formula/version and source links. A parser's successful extraction is not independent engineering validation.

For normalization, explicitly distinguish EUR/kW from EUR/MW, EUR/kWh from EUR/MWh, FOM percent-of-investment/year from absolute FOM and VOM EUR/MWh from fuel cost. For storage, either use a complete system cost or assemble power + energy + stated balance-of-system parts; never add a complete system quote to its own components. Connection/EPC/tax/insurance can already be inside another source total. Do not annualize an already annualized native `capital_cost` a second time. Rated process tonnes/day and MWh-product require a declared conversion/energy basis; HHV heat rate is not directly LHV efficiency.

Before promoting a seed pack: verify reuse terms and attribution; schema/units and currency/year checks; table-level extraction comparison; native cost/finance round-trip; hand-built cost decomposition and storage-duration cases; scenario/year/geography mismatch refusal; pin/update/local-override tests; and absence of false verified/quote labels. Fixed-point source checks must not rely on the same parser/calculation as their oracle.

Lifecycle validation must use physical calendar time and chronological/repeated duty, not optimization weights as elapsed age. An expected economic life is not MTTF or an outage frequency. Cell energy fade is not automatically pack usable-power loss, whole-BESS availability, warranty coverage or a field replacement schedule. Library records must identify these as different parameter types.

## Gaps that should stay visible

No comprehensive freely reusable EU/NA library was established for current vendor-specific UPS/SST/GFM converters, complete DC cooling plants, mine/concentrator equipment, process restart energy, site construction/EPC, or customer maintenance and component replacement histories. Public reports can supply clearly labelled illustrative ranges; obtain actual manufacturer/site inputs for engineering decisions. Many salvage/recycling values depend on local contractual/liability conditions and cannot be inferred from battery commodity value alone.

Recommended order: **P0 provenance/normalization on current pack; ATB + CER + selected DEA updates; PNNL storage decomposition; P1 UK/PVSCM/EIA/H2A/process-map detail and licensed empirical ageing references; P2 Mexican COPAR discovery and more specialized sources.** Do not delay useful native library improvements until every country and asset type is covered.
