# RP-16 — a generated synthetic solar profile trips the p_max_pu outlier finding

Status: needs-triage
Type: bug
Baseline: master after #623 (RP-04b)
Raised by: the Profiles II session's hand-off, 2026-10-22

A synthetic solar profile, generated by the app's own defaults, raises the section-6 `p_max_pu` outlier
finding: "peaks at 0.883732, 14886x its own median of 5.93681e-05". To reproduce, take one solar
generator at 50N 8E with a 2023 hourly index and the synthetic weather source, then click Expert ›
Time Series › Generate defaults for all.

To decide: either the synthetic solar night values should be zero rather than tiny, or the outlier
rule's median should skip near-zero hours, or skip solar altogether (a solar series' median is
naturally near zero).

Acceptance (once decided): the same fixture raises no outlier finding, and a real outlier in a solar
series is still flagged.
