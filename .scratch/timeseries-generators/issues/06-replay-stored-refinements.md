## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Re-generating a refined series replays its stored refinements by default.

**Current behavior:**
The load, availability and series generate routes replay refinements only when the request body carries `refinements`. The editors never send them, and the `generate_load_profile`, `generate_availability_profile` and `generate_series_profile` chat tools take no such argument. So re-generating a refined series silently drops its ops.

**Desired behavior:**
- When the target's currently applied series carries a recorded `refinements` list, a re-generate for the same target and family replays that list by default, through the existing replay path (recorded `resolved_mw` and `connection` kept, and `connection_changed_since_refinement` warned).
- The caller can opt out explicitly (e.g. `replay_refinements: false`) from the route, the editor and the chat tools. An explicit `refinements` list in the body still wins.
- Replay warnings appear on the old→new card. A replay refusal names the op and its index, and the card offers to generate without the ops rather than failing the generate.
- A different family (e.g. an archetype load replaced by an upload) does not replay.

**Key interfaces:**
- The generate request bodies (`refinements: list | None`) gain the opt-out flag. The stored artifact's `refinements` and `based_on` are the source of the default.
- The three `generate_*` chat tools gain the same opt-out argument and report replay warnings in their result.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Re-generating a refined load from the editor, without passing ops, yields the refined series.
- [ ] The same from chat (`generate_load_profile`), without passing ops, yields the refined series.
- [ ] With the opt-out set, the unrefined series is generated.
- [ ] A replay refusal (e.g. a window op outside the new year) names the op and its index on the card.
- [ ] Tests cover the route default, the opt-out, the chat tool and the refusal.

**Out of scope:**
- Per-op undo (issue 05).
- Replaying across families.
