## Gap

Time Series' whole-tab upload (one file, many components) is the bulk counterpart of the editors' "Measured file" tab (UX reviewer, #586), but its success and its refusals end in a toast only.

## What to build

An in-place receipt under the tab: the components matched (each with old → new and "Open its editor ›" where one exists), the columns not matched and why, and a refusal's reason with the template as the fix.

## Done when

Uploading a file with two matching columns and one unknown one shows all three in place, with no toast as the only record.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** The Time Series whole-tab upload leaves an in-place receipt instead of a toast only.

**Current behavior:**
On the Time Series page, uploading one file for a tab (many components, one column each) reports success with `toast.success` and refusals with `toast.error` (e.g. "File did not contain a column named …"). Nothing stays on the page.

**Desired behavior:**
- After an upload, a receipt shows under the tab's upload zone and stays until the next upload or until it is dismissed:
  - each component matched, with old → new (e.g. peak or mean, in the editor's plain words and units), and "Open its editor ›" where the component has a series editor;
  - each column not matched, with why (no such component; a component of another kind; a duplicate column);
  - for a refusal of the whole file, its reason and the template download as the fix.
- A toast may still announce the result, but it is never the only record.
- The receipt uses the editors' plain field words (`SeriesKit.fieldLabel`) and the existing card/receipt styling. No new design tokens.
- If the upload response does not say which columns were unmatched, extend it (backend) to list them, instead of inferring client-side.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Uploading a file with two matching columns and one unknown column shows all three in place: two matched rows with old → new, and one unmatched row with its reason.
- [ ] A refused file shows its reason and a template link in place.
- [ ] "Open its editor ›" opens that component's series editor.
- [ ] A frontend test covers the mixed file. A backend test covers the unmatched list if the response changed.

**Out of scope:**
- The per-editor "Measured file" tab.
- Changing the accepted file formats.
