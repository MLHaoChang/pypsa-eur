## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Report a power series' peak as a share of the grid connection.

**Current behavior:**
`scale_peak_to` with `of: "connection"` resolves the point-of-connection link's `p_nom` server-side. The refine stats report `peak_before`/`peak_after` in MW only, and the preview summary and the old→new card show no share.

**Desired behavior:**
- For a power target (`p_set`, or `p_max_pu` scaled by `p_nom`), when the project has a point-of-connection link, the refine stats carry the aligned peak's share of that link's `p_nom` (e.g. `peak_share_of_connection`, plus the link's display name).
- The preview summary in the series editors shows it as "80 % of the grid connection" (the link's display name in the tooltip or label).
- With no point-of-connection link, or `p_nom` = 0, nothing is shown. No error, no "0 %".
- The connection is resolved the same way `scale_peak_to` resolves it. Do not add a second lookup.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A load at 80 MW under a 100 MW connection reads "80 % of the grid connection" in the preview and carries 0.8 in the refine stats.
- [ ] With no connection link, the stats omit the field and the preview omits the line.
- [ ] One backend test and one frontend test.

**Out of scope:**
- The load-factor basis (issue 09).
- Showing the share on non-power targets (prices, COP).
