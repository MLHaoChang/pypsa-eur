- FMPy and OpenDSSDirect.py automatic license detection was NOASSERTION; actual files were inspected instead.
- oemof/demandlib redirects to oemof/oemof-demand in the retrieved metadata. Do not assume the historical install/import name matches a selected current release.
- pypsa-ppa declares proof-of-concept status. Financial/source claims are not a substitute for GridSpine reference tests.
