* Feat(pypsa-gui): The staged hydrogen hub and island microgrid examples can be created for any planning years, as the staged data center can. `POST /api/projects/from_template/eh_h2_hub_staged` and `/eh_microgrid_staged` take `{"planning": {"start_year", "end_year", "dates"?}}`. The investment periods, their weights, the finance horizon, the tariff and the island sidecar's candidates follow the entered years. An H2 offtake or microgrid load scale the example has no figure for in an entered year is the example's same-stage value, marked a placeholder: the flow warns about it, and the investment case flags it. The microgrid's peak check reads that period as unknown; the H2 offtake is not an electric load, so the hub's peak check is unchanged. A start before 2027 holds the CO2 price's first point (80 EUR/t) from the start year, recorded in the ledger. Without `planning`, both templates are unchanged (E1b).
