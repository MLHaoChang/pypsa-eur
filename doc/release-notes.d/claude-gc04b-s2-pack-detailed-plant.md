* Feat(gridspine): The PSS/E study pack handles a detailed plant (GC-04b): `Plant(..., members=...)`. Every converter of the plant gets a gain-shared REPCA1. The psspy scripts step every machine's reference by the same reference MW (each machine's output moves by the step in its own pu), sum the machines' currents by MBASE, and poll every machine for `connected`. The pack lists unequal per-unit dispatch among its assumptions, and refuses a plant whose converters carry different plant-controller values. All plant-controller values stay assumed (generic WECC), and the record layouts and psspy calls remain unverified on PSS/E.
