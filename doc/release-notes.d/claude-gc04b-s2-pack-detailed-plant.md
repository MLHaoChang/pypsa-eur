* Feat(gridspine): The PSS/E study pack handles a detailed plant (GC-04b): `Plant(..., members=...)`. Every converter of the plant gets a gain-shared REPCA1. The psspy scripts step every machine's reference by the same MW, sum the machines' currents by MBASE, and poll every machine for `connected`. The pack lists unequal per-unit dispatch among its assumptions, and refuses a plant whose converters carry different plant-controller values.
