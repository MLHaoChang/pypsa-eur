* Fix(pypsa-gui): Rolling-horizon and myopic solves now persist the largest PCS oversize and STATCOM size over all their windows or periods, not just the last one. The cost breakdown counts that investment once, at the largest size. On a multi-period network each investment period is charged for the size it adds over the periods before it, so the cost now appears in the per-period view. The PCS/STATCOM rows no longer crash on snapshots that carry an investment period.
