* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant can give checked dispatch figures for one Store (`store-dispatch/1`, opt-in): the energy charged, discharged and net, the standing loss, the peak charging and discharging power, the stored energy at the end, and the peak fill of the optimised capacity, for the whole run or an exact window. It follows PyPSA's Store balance and sign convention, uses the store snapshot weights, and reads the store's time-varying standing loss and energy bounds where it has them. Every figure is withheld, with a named reason, when the energy does not follow the balance or leaves its bounds, or the standing loss is outside 0 to 1. Only the loss is withheld for a negative energy under a standing loss, and only the fill when no optimised capacity is available.
