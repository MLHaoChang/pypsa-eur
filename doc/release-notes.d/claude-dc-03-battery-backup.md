* Feat(pypsa-gui): A data-centre project can switch its outage backup from gensets to batteries or a hybrid (`PUT /api/campus-electrical/{name}/dc-backup`), and assess it (`POST .../dc-backup/assess`). The assessment sizes the battery strings on the redundancy rule, proves both the gensets and the chosen mode over the outage window of largest critical energy, and prices the cost difference against the gensets over the analysis horizon. The setting leaves the saved network and the investment case untouched. The backup strings hold their charge for the outage and never shave the site peak. The Backup section of the Grid study shows the mode, the verdicts and the cost delta.
