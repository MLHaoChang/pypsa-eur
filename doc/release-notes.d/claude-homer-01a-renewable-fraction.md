* Feat(pypsa-gui): Minimum renewable fraction (HOMER parity). Solver Settings has a new "Off-grid constraints" block with an optional "Minimum renewable fraction (%)". When set, the LP adds one energy row per investment period: non-renewable electricity (diesel, gas, grid import, Link gensets fed from a fuel bus) may supply at most (1 − fraction) of all the electricity used on site: the load served plus what conversion units (electrolysers, heat pumps, e-boilers, process units) draw. Charging storage is not use, and a converter whose output both feeds a load and comes back to electricity makes the fraction unavailable rather than guessed. A target the renewables cannot reach is infeasible and the solve log names the row; with a VoLL slack the LP sheds load instead. The new `GET /api/results/renewable_fraction` reports the renewable fraction of every solve, per period, whether or not the constraint is on. Rolling and myopic solves refuse the target at preflight. Off by default; existing results and fingerprints are unchanged.
