* Feat(pypsa-gui): Generated renewable profiles follow the model's snapshots: with no weather year given, the snapshots' year is used when it is a completed past year (else the last completed year), and the profile records `year_source`. Whole-hour multi-hour snapshots (for example 3-hourly) now get the mean of the hourly weather series over each step, where they used to get the value at the step's first hour, so re-applying a profile on an existing multi-hour network writes different values. Irregular steps, non-whole-hour steps, a step across a local-clock DST gap, and profile families without a defined step mean are refused by name.
