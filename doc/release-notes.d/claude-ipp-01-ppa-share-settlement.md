* Feat(pypsa-gui): PPA and CfD contracts take a `share` of their assets' output. A pay-as-produced PPA the site sells can settle as `financial`: (price − ref) × share × gen, through the CfD difference code, suspended at negative prices by default. That form is no longer a double count with the export price. Preflight warns on basis risk (a reference other than the export series) and on a financial PPA that pays through negative prices. The contract editor edits the new fields. The investment case's break-even PPA price works on a financial PPA.

* Fix(pypsa-gui): the investment case now escalates a CfD's (and a financial PPA's) two legs apart over the analysis years. The strike/price leg moves at the contract's indexation (else `ppa`), and the reference leg at `export`, like the market revenue it offsets. Before, the whole difference moved at the indexation. **Existing CfD results change** in the years after the modelled one whenever the indexation differs from the export escalation. For example, at 2 % indexation and 0 % export escalation, a year-10 line of 70 € strike vs 60 € reference on 700 MWh goes from 8,365.65 to 16,559.54.
