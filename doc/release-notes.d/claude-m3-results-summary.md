* Feat(pypsa-gui): Results opens on a Summary tab after each solve: the total system cost with its capital, operating and new-build parts, the CO₂ emitted and whether a cap binds (with its price), the unserved energy and loss-of-load hours (or that lost load was not modelled), the reserve margin or reliability target when one was set, and the largest capacities with what the solve built. Each card links to its detailed tab; the tab you pick is kept until the next solve. The assistant can open the Summary tab.
