* Feat(pypsa-gui): Study harness profile `eh_site@6` takes a URDB tariff the chat intake cites as an uploaded OpenEI JSON. The grant pins the upload, every import argument and the tariff's Library hash, and the inputs stage imports and attaches it unattended. A tariff whose currency differs from the project's, or whose validity misses a modelled year, is refused at the grant and again at use. Earlier `eh_site` versions are unchanged.
