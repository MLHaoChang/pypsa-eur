* Feat(pypsa-gui): The adequacy screens read split (Store + charger/discharger Link) storage like the equal StorageUnit: the ride-through, smoothing, portfolio fingerprint, redundancy and storage-duration lever, readiness and activity readers. A split storage is in service only while its charger, discharger and Store all are, and one whose charger and discharger sit on different buses is named, not counted.

* Change(pypsa-gui): The adequacy Monte Carlo now derates a StorageUnit by its `p_max_pu` and `-p_min_pu` (static or time-varying), as it does a split storage's Links. A StorageUnit that cannot charge or discharge (for example `p_min_pu = 0`) is now named in the storage notes and not counted; default limits give the same results as before. An inflow-fed StorageUnit (reservoir hydro, `p_min_pu = 0` with inflow) keeps its dispatch power, since the Monte Carlo models no inflow, and the storage notes say so.
