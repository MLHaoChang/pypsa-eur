* Feat(pypsa-gui): A checked answer can now cover every generator (generator dispatch) or every line (branch loading) in one request, for example "show me the key results for every generator". The server lists the entities from the network, records that list in the evidence, and splits answers above 20 values into at most five headed sections ("Generators 21–30 of 30"). Each section's numbers are checked against that section's facts only. A request above 100 values is refused before any calculation runs. The receipt gains policy "4" with a `sections` field; the chat renderer comes in a follow-up.
