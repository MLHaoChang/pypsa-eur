* Feat(pypsa-gui): Business case route and workbook sheet (business case 08a). The business-case inputs are stored with the project (`GET`/`PUT /api/results/business_case/inputs`, If-Match). They are in no solve fingerprint and no investment-case key. `GET /api/results/business_case/preset` offers the template's preset with cost defaults; a pack row in another unit is a 422 `default_unit_mismatch`. `POST /api/results/business_case` runs the business case on the current investment case (409 `investment_case_missing` otherwise) as an abortable study. Linked outputs and lost production come from that case's own solve. `GET` returns the P&L, KPIs and break-even, and says stale when its inputs or the investment case change. The results workbook gains a "Business case" sheet: `not_established` before a run, labelled stale without figures after an edit.
