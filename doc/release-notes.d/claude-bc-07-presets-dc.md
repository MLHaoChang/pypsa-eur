* Feat(pypsa-gui): Business case presets (BC-ALL slice 07). Versioned preset files under `project_templates/business_case_presets/` pre-fill the line table. The two data-centre templates get a GPU-cloud preset (GPU-hours, which require facility and IT hardware) and a colocation preset (kW-month lease and power pass-through). Their cost rows take sourced pack 2026-11-10 defaults, marked assumed with a source and range. `eh_site` keeps a blank table with ten offered rows. Revenue rows show a public reference range as a hint, never a value. Lines gain the `MW` unit (typed capex only), `linked_share` for the share of a linked output sold under the line (a lost-production drop scales with it), and `default_units`: a pack row in another unit is refused `default_unit_mismatch`. Existing business-case digests are unchanged.
