* Feat(pypsa-gui): The results workbook gets sheets for the Adequacy and FMEA result views, read from the same functions as their tabs: the energy target and achieved energy not served per investment period and over the horizon, by zone, and the reserve margin per period; and every computed failure mode with its occurrence, ΔEUE, criticality and severity, each with native Excel charts. Each failure mode's criticality and severity are labelled by their own basis: per study horizon for class A and B, and per year (per event for severity) for class C. The "highest criticality" is given per basis, and the sheet states the horizon length. Each sensitivity run gets its own column, read from its own run.
