* Feat(pypsa-gui): The add-asset form no longer asks for a capital cost (an annuity). A generator, link, store, transformer or line is priced by its overnight cost and lifetime, in the same units as its Expert card: €/kW, €/kWh, €/MVA, or €/MVA per km for a line. A battery or other storage unit is priced on its card, as its two parts. Efficiencies are entered in %, and labels follow the cards: for example "Maximum capacity", not "P nom max".
