* Feat(pypsa-gui): Regional technology-cost packs for the generic defaults pack: Canada (CER Energy Future 2026, Ontario, scenario CM, 2030, CAD), the UK (DESNZ Electricity Generation Costs 2025, 2030 commissioning, GBP) and the US (EIA AEO2025 reference plants, USD). A region is chosen explicitly (`load_defaults_pack(region=...)`, `price_from_pack(..., region=...)`), a money row a region lacks raises instead of borrowing the EU row, and EU projects and pack hashes are unchanged.
