* Feat(pypsa-gui): A load's series is now generated in one editor, the same in the load's Properties panel and in the Time Series drawer: pick an archetype or preset and a peak or annual energy, or read a measured meter file, then preview the peak, the year's energy and what the model's snapshots count before applying. Expert opens the archetype's parameters and the refinement edits; Guided keeps them under Advanced. The per-row load upload in Time Series now opens this editor.

* Feat(pypsa-gui): Generators and links get an Availability editor, in their Properties panel and in the Time Series drawer (the Conventional and Links rows): planned maintenance by season or dated windows, a must-run floor (constant or winter/summer) and temperature derating, previewed before Apply. Forced outages are shown read-only, sampled in the reliability study rather than the dispatch, with a link to that study.

* Feat(pypsa-gui): A price series (peak and off-peak, or an ordered period list, with its currency, money basis and year) can be generated for a generator's or link's marginal cost, in Properties and from the Time Series marginal-cost rows; a storage unit gets an inflow editor (annual energy with a snowmelt, rain-fed or flat shape, or monthly shares) and a heat-pump link a COP editor (air or ground source, supply temperature).

* Feat(pypsa-gui): Every series editor shows what the component carries now (an upload as measured, a generated series with its kind) and can refine it: the current series becomes a pending series on the clock it is stored on, so applying it unchanged writes back the same values. On a snapshot window, shifts and smoothing are refused and an energy target is the window's MWh. Apply refuses it once the stored series has changed since.
