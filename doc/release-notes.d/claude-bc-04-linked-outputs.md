* Feat(pypsa-gui): Business-case lines can link their quantity to an output of the project's own solve. The new catalogue (`services/business_case/linked_outputs.py`) offers, per project, the product served on a product bus, hydrogen delivered, concentrate shipped, oxygen co-produced (derived), electric load served, energy exported at the grid side of the export link, capacity in service, and the data centre's served IT energy and contracted kW, each per year of an investment period and with its unit conversions (H2 MWh to kg by LHV, MWh to t by the bus's `mwh_per_t`, MWh to GPU-hours by kW per GPU). A stale, unsolved or unreadable solve reads "not evaluated", never zero. Two refusals join the line-item model: `linked_output_missing` and `double_count`. Nothing here enters the LP, the investment case or the adequacy facts.
