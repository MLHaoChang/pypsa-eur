* Fix(pypsa-gui): A plan step whose job was stopped on purpose (`cancel_job`, `abort_simulation`, `solve_queue_abort` or a study's abort) now settles with step status `cancelled` instead of `failed`, and the plan card says "stopped". A queued step reads cancelled only when every one of its jobs was stopped. The job's own outcome stays `aborted`, no continuation follows, and the step can be retried like a failed one (`resolve_task_step(retry)`, counting toward the retry cap). Task files saved before this keep reading `failed`.
