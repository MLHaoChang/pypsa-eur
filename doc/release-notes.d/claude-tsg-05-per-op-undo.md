* Feat(pypsa-gui): A refined time series lists its refinement steps, and each step can be removed on its own. Removing a step builds a new preview without it behind the same old → new card, and the copilot does the same with ``undo_index`` on ``refine_generated_profile``. A later step that no longer fits is refused by name. The change log of an applied refinement lists each step with its parameters.
