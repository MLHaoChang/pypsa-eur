* Feat(pypsa-gui): Study harness profile `eh_site@5` adds the genset fuel price as a research gap. If the grant allows research, the run can propose a cited and verified price stated in EUR per MWh of genset electrical output. Once the user confirms it, one carded edit writes it to the site's gensets (the `genset*` units). Gensets whose fuel curve sets their cost are left out, and so are other generators on the same fuel, such as a CHP or a fuel supply; the report names them. A price in another unit, a quote that prices fuel energy, or a site whose gensets burn two fuels is refused. A run that does not research the price keeps the template's value, and the report gives the reason. Profiles `eh_site@1` to `@4` are unchanged.
