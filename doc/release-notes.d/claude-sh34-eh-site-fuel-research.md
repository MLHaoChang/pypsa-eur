* Feat(pypsa-gui): Study harness profile `eh_site@5` adds the genset fuel price as a research gap. If the grant allows research, a cited and verified price in EUR/MWh of genset electrical output can be proposed. Once the user confirms it, one carded edit writes it to every genset of the site's single fuel carrier. A price in another unit, or a site whose gensets burn two fuels, is refused. Gensets whose fuel curve sets their cost are left out. A run that does not research the price keeps the template's value, and the report names the reason. Profiles `eh_site@1` to `@4` are unchanged.
