* Feat(pypsa-gui): Results workbook, first package. `GET /api/results/workbook.xlsx` exports the run as one Excel file: About, an Overview of investment-grade KPIs per investment period and over the horizon (overnight and annualised capex, opex and its parts, total system cost, energy served, system and per-carrier LCOE, and the investment case's NPV, IRR, payback, DSCR, financial LCOE and debt size, or `not_established` with "run the investment case"), native Excel charts drawn from the sheet's own cells, live NPV formulas, and the investment-case sheets. Views follow the project's selected cards plus `views=` or `all=`; a stale run is refused unless `stored=true`. When the project has sensitivity runs, a Scenarios sheet after About sets each run the user may open beside the base (key KPIs over the horizon and per period, investment-case KPIs where current, Compare-view headline figures, live deltas against the base, two native charts), says why a run cannot be compared, and counts the runs not shown.
