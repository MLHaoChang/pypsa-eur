* Feat(gridspine): A campus description can hold series capacitors (`series_capacitors`: `xc_ohm`, or the compensation degree `k` of the cable in series with it). Each enters the net as a negative-reactance impedance and reaches the sub-synchronous scan as `series_caps`. Fault-level and grid-strength paths short a bypassed capacitor, as IEC 60909-0 allows. Code that walks only lines and transformers (contingency and planning screens, island and sequence fault solvers, the RAW and PyPSA writers) refuses such a campus and names the capacitor.
