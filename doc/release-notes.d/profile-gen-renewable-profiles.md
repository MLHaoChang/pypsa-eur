* Feat(pypsa-gui): Renewable profile generator — wind/solar capacity-factor profiles from lon/lat points, synthetic (deterministic, no download) or ERA5/atlite (cached cutouts, CDS credentials). Generation runs as a `profile_gen` job in the solve queue; pending artifacts are applied onto a generator's `p_max_pu` from the new Profile Studio panel (BUILD) or the assistant's six profile tools.

* Feat(pypsa-gui): Applying a generated profile takes a ``time_basis`` (``UTC`` or an IANA zone whose wall clock the snapshots are; the UTC weather series is then shifted by that zone's offset, DST included). Unstated, it follows the commercial-tariff rule: UTC when the project's commercial timezone is set, otherwise the profile is applied unshifted, recorded as an assumption and returned with a warning to set the time zone. The basis is recorded in the profile's metadata. The partial-year refusal now explains what to do on representative-week templates.
