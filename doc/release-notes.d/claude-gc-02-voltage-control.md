* Feat(gridspine): Voltage-control strategy in the campus load flow (TRGC 12). The plant power controller runs fixed Q, power-factor, PoC-voltage or Q(V)-droop control, shares reactive power across units in proportion to their available Q, and reports when they reach a limit. It solves against the grid's Thevenin equivalent at both S_k''min and S_k''max. A PoC P–Q chart covers 0.90–1.10 pu and is judged against the profile's Q band, and the scorecard gains voltage-control rows. New optional profile keys: `q_v_slope_pct`, `voltage_control_modes` and `q_band_v_range_pu`. When a key is absent its row reads unknown, never pass.
