* Feat(pypsa-gui): Study harness profile `eh_site@4` runs the investment case after the solve. The case spends no solve, and its wait ends at the run's wall time with the typed refusal `ic_wall_time`. The hand-off reads the stored case's project NPV at WACC and its IRRs, and reads a stale case as `not_current`. A case that ends refused or failed, or runs past the run's wall time, leaves the investment chapter as a listed gap with its error instead of stopping the run. `eh_site@1`–`@3` are unchanged.
