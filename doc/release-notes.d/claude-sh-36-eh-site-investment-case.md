* Feat(pypsa-gui): Study harness profile `eh_site@4` runs the investment case at the head of the report stage, so it values the final dispatch after the result gate's loop. The run's grant reserves the two solves of the case's baseline at admission (budget 6 → 8). With fewer than two solves left, the case is refused `ic_budget` and runs no LP. The case's wait ends at the run's wall time with the typed refusal `ic_wall_time`. The hand-off reads the stored case's project NPV at WACC and its IRRs, and reads a stale case as `not_current`. A case that ends refused or failed, or runs past the wall time, leaves the investment chapter as a listed gap with its kind instead of stopping the run. `eh_site@1`–`@3` are unchanged.
