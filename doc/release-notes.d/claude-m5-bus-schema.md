* Feat(pypsa-gui): Buses, carriers, global constraints and shunt impedances are described by the asset schema, like the assets. The Bus panel's read-out and edit form now take their labels, units and help from it ("Nominal voltage (kV)", "Longitude (°E)", the control options PQ / PV / Slack), and the Buses and Carriers tables take their column headers from it. A bus's country or sub-network can be cleared, and an edited extra column on a bus is now saved. The assistant's create tool lists the attributes of all four.
