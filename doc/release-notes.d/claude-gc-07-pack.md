* Feat(gridspine): Pack read-back judges the per-case results of a PSCAD/EMT test pack (PSCAD `.inf`/`.out` or CSV per case id) against the grid-code profile and writes `compliance/judgment.json` and `judgment.csv` into the run directory; the battery is bound to the pack manifest by its SHA-256, MW and Mvar are put in pu of the plant ratings with their stated source, and a missing result, channel or rating reads "unknown", never "pass". Fault cases read "unknown" until the channel contract carries a breaker / trip status channel.
