* Feat(pypsa-gui): A checked answer can state a derived figure (difference, sum, percent change, percentage-point change, share, or a kW/MW/GW or kWh/MWh conversion) over the numbers it publishes. The software recomputes each one and shows only its own value; an unknown formula, an unpublished operand, operands from another run, window or cost basis, power against energy, MVA as MW, a near-zero denominator or a value off by more than 1e-9 relative limits the answer.
