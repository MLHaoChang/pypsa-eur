* Feat(gridspine): Import a TSO contingency list (TRGC 15) from a PSS/E ``.con`` subset or the ``contingencies.csv`` form. Elements are mapped onto the campus net by RAW bus number or name, circuit and machine id, and the result feeds ``screen_planning`` unchanged. Unknown, ambiguous and unsupported elements are listed with their line numbers. Their contingencies are left out whole rather than screened as a smaller outage.
