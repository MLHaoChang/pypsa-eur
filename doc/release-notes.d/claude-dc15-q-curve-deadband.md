* Feat(gridspine, pypsa-gui): A grid-code profile can state a per-unit reactive capability curve (`q_capability_unit`, Q against P per unit of the unit's rated active power) and a voltage-control deadband (`voltage_deadband_pct`). The campus study checks each inverter's circle against the curve at its breakpoints and at rated P in a new `campus_ibr_q_curve.csv` (a unit without a rating is "capability unknown", never pass); the deadband is reported, not checked. Both are optional with no default, and the grid-code profile section shows and confirms them like the other limits.
