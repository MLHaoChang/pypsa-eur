* Feat(pypsa-gui): The renewables IPP example ships two observed 2024 day-ahead price years: DE-LU from SMARD (Bundesnetzagentur) and DK1 from Energinet, both CC BY 4.0 and attributed. They sit beside the illustrative shapes in the Library and fill nothing by default. They are re-dated to the 2030 calendar (29 Feb dropped, weekdays shift by one day) and taken as 2030 real money; the ledger states this assumption. They were fetched once by a script, the files are pinned by hash, and nothing is fetched at run time.
