* Feat(pypsa-gui): The renewable fraction now counts a hydrogen (or other) cluster that both feeds a load and returns electricity, such as an electrolyser and a fuel cell on an H2 bus with an offtake, by its net draw: what the electrolysers take from electricity minus what the fuel cell gives back, per period, the same number in the KPI and in the minimum-renewable-fraction row. Before, such a cluster made the fraction unavailable. A cluster that can return energy it never drew (a store that starts full, or a fuel-fed link delivering into it) and an unknown efficiency still make the fraction unavailable rather than guessed.
