* Feature(pypsa-gui): A checked assistant answer can now cite the peak or mean of the summed load profile from `get_aggregate_load`, for every load, one section or a list of names. The statistic is computed from every member's raw profile over every step, so it is the global value, never one page's or one load's. A partial sum is refused as `aggregate_not_checkable` before the tool runs: a load with no profile, a missing or non-finite step, an unknown or repeated name, or loads on different time indexes. Editing a member, or a load joining or leaving the selection, makes the evidence unavailable.
