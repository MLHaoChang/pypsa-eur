* Feat(pypsa-gui): The investment-case engine finances staged builds (P5b): each owner asset can have its own COD (`FinanceCase.asset_cod`); a later stage's capex is paid `stage_capex_lead_years` before its COD, escalated and with contingency, equity-funded (outside debt sizing, CFADS and the cover ratios), depreciated from its own COD, and shown in the report as `stage_capex`; degradation, replacements, the lifetime check, the remaining-life terminal value and the storage LCOS run on each asset's own clock. Incentives or dated tax schedules on a later stage, stage CODs off the operating axis, capex before financial close, and a replacement dated before its asset's COD are refused. Cases without stages keep their hash and their results, with one fix: a replacement of an asset with no upfront basis (overnight cost 0) whose own depreciation classes carry a zero share is now depreciated on those classes; before, its depreciation was dropped.
