* Feat(pypsa-gui): Business case: a per-year lease-up volume factor (`volume_factor_by_year`, 0–100 %, default 100 %). A year below full volume scales the linked served-volume lines and the energy model's dispatched-volume rows together, pro rata (the dispatch is not re-solved); fixed rows and installed capacity stay. A typed revenue or opex line (`volume_factor_typed_line`) and a generation-side row such as export or a PPA (`volume_factor_unclassified`) refuse rather than guess. The report and workbook state the factor per year, its basis and any stated year outside the operating years. At 100 % every result and hash is unchanged.
