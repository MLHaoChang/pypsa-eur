* Feat(pypsa-gui): PyPSA Studio now opens in a light theme by default: white surfaces, a red accent and pale-red tinted bands. It follows the operating system's dark-mode setting until you pick a theme yourself, and the red-on-black dark theme stays one click away in the sidebar and the command palette. The sign-in page follows the same choice. Both themes meet WCAG AA contrast, pinned by tests.
