* Feat(pypsa-gui): Guided can now create your own site. "Your own site" on the Guided Start card, also reachable as a card in the New Project wizard's From template tab, opens a form for a generic site that is not a data center. Pick its type (industrial plant, commercial building, hospital, university or campus, other), and every value starts from that type's preset, tagged "default" until you change it. The form covers the load per growth stage, the grid connection and what is available in each year, the gensets, PV, battery and UPS on site, and what the study may add. Problems show beside the field in plain words, including a peak above the grid available that year. The Site card then shows the load and connection schedule read-only, the must-stay-on load as entered, and "Start a new project from these values".
