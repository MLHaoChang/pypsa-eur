* Feat(pypsa-gui): The in-app assistant can keep working toward a goal over several turns without being asked each time (`set_goal`, default 5 rounds, at most 20). A goal strip above the composer shows the goal and its rounds, with Pause, Resume and Stop. Rounds stop when the goal is reached or something needs you: a card to approve, a question, an error or a usage limit. Every change still asks for approval as in a normal turn, and only you can resume a paused goal.
