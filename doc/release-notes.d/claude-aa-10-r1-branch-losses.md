* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant can give checked transmission-loss figures (`branch-losses/1`): loss energy, the largest single-branch loss, served demand and losses as a share of demand. They are read from the solve's raw loss values before any zero-fill. A branch or load with a missing, infinite, absent or misaligned value gives no figure, and the reply names its state instead of reporting 0 MWh.
