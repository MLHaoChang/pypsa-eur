* Feat(pypsa-gui): Multi-objective front engine for the data-centre templates (`services/adequacy/front.py`): sweeps a reliability lever (ENS cap or reserve margin, chosen from whether the dispatch model has outages) against a CO2 cap. Each point is re-optimised and records annualised capex and opex, CO2 and its shadow price, and the built capacities. Each point also carries a sampled-outage LOLE/EUE taken with the same seed and number of draws. Dominated points are marked, a budget refuses oversized grids before any solve, and an axis that never binds is said plainly.
