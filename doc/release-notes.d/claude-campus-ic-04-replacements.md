* Feat(pypsa-gui): The investment case takes the grid study's planned replacements of campus equipment (Commercial & finance > Investment case). Each renewal is bought once, in its build year less the stage capex lead, under either replacement rule, and the terminal value counts from the last one. The part-lifetimes rule no longer buys a campus asset a second time; it resumes only after the grid study's last purchase reaches the end of its life inside the case's horizon, which is flagged `campus_life_ends_unreplaced`. When an existing asset is first renewed outside an investment period, it is taken as a later stage and pays no fixed O&M before its build year. A renewal after the horizon is left out and flagged `campus_renewal_after_horizon`. A `replacement_capex` entry for a campus asset is refused. Stored investment case reports with a campus study read stale once.
