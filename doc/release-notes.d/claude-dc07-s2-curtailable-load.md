* Feat(pypsa-gui): A data-centre `dc_load` spec can set `curtailment_price_eur_per_mwh`: the solve may then curtail up to `curtailable_share` of the facility load on the cooling bus at that price, reported apart from unserved energy. With `curtail_max_duration_h` the curtailed energy is bounded per run of that many hours (an energy proxy: curtailment can still be spread; consecutive hours are not enforced). The curtailed MW per snapshot is saved with the result; energy flows, load shape and the CFE score count only the served load and show the curtailed energy apart. A rolling solve with `curtail_max_duration_h` is refused for now (`duration_under_rolling_not_supported`).
