* Feat(pypsa-gui): The green-ammonia template's investment case now computes against buying grey NH3. A product's buy-in and shortfall Generators (found by `process_role`) are no longer owner assets: the buy-in is booked as a `fuel` purchase from the market (its CO2 included once, in its cost series), and the shortfall as a new `lost_production` stream owed to the offtaker, at the lost-production value, never VoLL. The value-flow templates move up one version, and the bundled templates' value flows are regenerated.
