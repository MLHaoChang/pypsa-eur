* Feat(gridspine): PowerFactory handoff for the power-quality and small-signal studies (TRGC 13, 14, 18). `powerfactory_campus` writes the campus network per island hour as an ASCII DGS file: terminals, lines and cables, 2-winding transformers with their tap, loads, static generators, synchronous machines, shunts, and the external grid at S_k min and max. Each converter carries the harmonic current spectrum the harmonic screen uses, with the IEC 61000-3-6 summation exponents. A converter with no spectrum is listed. A field measurement of the background voltage harmonics at the PCC (95th-percentile values per order) can be imported with its source and date. The harmonic screen then adds a "with background" row summed per IEC 61000-3-6. `read_back` re-keys a PowerFactory load-flow export by DGS names onto the hour's pandapower load flow.
