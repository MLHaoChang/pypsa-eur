* Feat(gridspine): The PLEXOS importer reads dated rows (Date From/To) and Timeslice-tagged rows as step series over the snapshots, and the `NAME,M1..M12`, `Month,Day,Period` and `Pattern` data-file layouts. A gap, an overlap with different values, a date inside a snapshot, an unreadable timeslice pattern, or a static property that changes within the horizon leaves the property unmapped with the reason; nothing is filled or rounded.
