* Feat(gridspine): P–V and Q–V voltage-stability margins at the point of connection (`gridspine/static/voltage_stability.py`). The P–V nose is found by stepping and then bisecting, with a flat-start retry. The Q–V sweep uses a fictitious PV source to give the minimum-Q margin and dQ/dV. Cases are intact plus the worst N-1 outages by PoC voltage (default 5). Every row is labelled "screening; binding answer is the RMS study".
