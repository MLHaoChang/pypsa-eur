* Feat(pypsa-gui): The Energy Hub study gains an opt-in `front` stage after the frontier. It runs the cost / reliability / CO2 front on the pack-applied study copy with the study's MC seed and draws, on the ENS lever around the pack's ENS target or on the reserve-margin lever around the session's margin (an assumed 0.10 when none is set, named in the result). Its result is the `eh_front` table beside the report. No pack runs it by default, and a study that does not request it reports exactly as before.
