* Feat(pypsa-gui): A chat-started solve now keeps a bounded digest of its Prices results with the run (`services/results/prices_digest.py`): overall, per bus carrier and per bus the objective-weighted mean, median, p90, max with its snapshot and min of the merit-order-corrected prices, and the load-weighted mean (null with a reason when there is no load), with the `price_drivers` payload kept beside it. `get_results(result_kind='prices' | 'price_drivers', run_id=…)` answers it for the current and for an older run alike, and refuses a source or paging argument. Multi-period networks are not digested.
