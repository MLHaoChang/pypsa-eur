* Feat(pypsa-gui): Continuous speech (LC-07, off by default) now ignores a final transcript that repeats, word for word, at least three words of the answer being read out while it may still be audible. The assistant's own voice picked up by the microphone therefore never becomes a new request. Overlapping finals during a running turn queue one correction, never a second turn. The owner's `GET /speech` lists transcript-free notices for finals that were not admitted.
