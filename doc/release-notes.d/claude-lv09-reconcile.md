* Feat(pypsa-gui): After a restart, a durable assistant conversation can settle its unverified effects (`POST /api/durable-conversations/{id}/reconcile`). Jobs are verified only by reading the solve queue as the conversation's owner, and a foreign, unknown or no-longer-visible job looks the same. The owner marks interrupted turns and tool calls done, not done or dismissed. A request applies whole or not at all, and nothing is replayed.
