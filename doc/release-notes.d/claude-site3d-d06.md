* Fix(pypsa-gui): A 3D site placement or footprint now belongs to one component instance, not just its name. A component deleted and recreated under the same name, replaced by an imported network or rebuilt by clustering no longer inherits the old position, heading or footprint, even when a sidecar hook failed. Undo of a rename or delete brings the placement back, and Save-As and scenario copies keep it. A placement bound since the project was last saved can show as unbound after a scenario branch, a snapshot, a Save-As (in the source project) or a discarded session; it stays in the project and is listed as unbound.
