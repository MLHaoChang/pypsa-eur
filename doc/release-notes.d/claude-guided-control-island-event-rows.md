* Fix(pypsa-gui): Seamless islanding now also keeps headroom for the island's own events, the ones the frequency check judges: the largest load step (a load, or a charging battery's draw), the trip of any one unit (a charging battery's included), and the loss of the PV. Before, it kept headroom for the islanding step only. The new I2b rows are `Island-e2-up`, `Island-n1-up/-down-<unit>` and `Island-e5-up`.
  - This can raise battery sizes or genset commitment on any seamless site, including the generic site with `ride_through` on.
  - A site can now be infeasible when one load, or a charging battery's draw, arriving as a step exceeds what its grid formers and its gensets' load step can pick up. The solver's infeasibility hints name the island rows and, for such a step, the missing MW.
  - On the staged microgrid the GFM settings recommender now reaches a recommendation at the Guided default ranking (k=3), and the demo's pre-tax NPV moves from +43.9 M to +43.2 M EUR.
