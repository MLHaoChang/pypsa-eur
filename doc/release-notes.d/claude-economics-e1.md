* Feat(pypsa-gui): The Expert sidebar has an Economics step between Time and Run. Its panel gathers the inputs that decide what the model charges. The Basis tab holds the discount and inflation rates, and the money year and price basis. The Asset costs tab lists every generator, link, storage unit, store, line and transformer, with its overnight cost per kW (per MVA for lines and transformers), its lifetime and the annualised capital cost the model charges. It also shows where each cost came from, and which assets are not priced. A row opens the asset's card, where its cost is edited. The assistant can open the panel too.
