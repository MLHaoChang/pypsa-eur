* Feat(pypsa-gui): The Expert sidebar has an Economics step between Time and Run, with one panel for the inputs that decide what the model charges. The Basis tab holds the discount and inflation rates, the site's connection to the grid (which can now be set before any solve), and the money year and price basis. The Asset costs tab lists every generator, link, storage unit, store, line and transformer with its overnight cost per kW (per MVA for lines and transformers), its lifetime, the annualised capital cost the model charges and where the cost came from, and says which assets are not priced. A row opens the asset's card, where its cost is edited. The Tariffs & contracts tab holds the import tariff, contracts and participants, and the Library tab the tariff and series library. The Results → Investment tab keeps its views, and the assistant can open the panel.
