* Feat(pypsa-gui): A fuel-burning generator takes a HOMER-style fuel curve: a fuel price, a slope (fuel per MWh generated) and a no-load fuel per hour per MW of capacity while the unit is on. The curve sets the generator's marginal cost and stand-by cost, so a genset idling at low load now pays for the fuel it burns; no-load fuel needs Unit commitment. The cost breakdown lists each curve generator's fuel burn and cost, and minimum output reads as the minimum load ratio when the unit is committed. Every solve, the sizing loop included, refuses a curve it cannot price. Clearing a curve says what happens to the costs. The DC backup assessment notes that it prices genset fuel from its own heat rate, not from a curve. Generators without a curve are unchanged.
