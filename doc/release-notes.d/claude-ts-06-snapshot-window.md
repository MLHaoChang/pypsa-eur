* Feat(pypsa-gui): Generated series now apply to networks whose snapshots sample the year (an Energy Hub study week, several sampled weeks, a month): the weather or source year is sliced to the snapshot window by month-day-hour and the window is recorded with the series. A power series' energy is checked against what the window's weightings claim to represent, per investment period, and flagged when a sampled week does not represent the year. An hour the source does not have (Feb 29, a skipped daylight-saving hour) is refused, never filled.
