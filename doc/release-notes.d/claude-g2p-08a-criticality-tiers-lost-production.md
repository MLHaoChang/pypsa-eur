* Feat(pypsa-gui): Grid-to-process criticality tiers and lost-production value (ticket 08a). A product bus's new `lost_production_value` adds a transient `__lost_<bus>` shortfall slack at solve time, priced at that value and never at VoLL; a hand-built shortfall Generator still wins. Its dispatch is captured into the solve's lost-load record, saved and reloaded with the project, and read as stale after an edit. The process view, energy flows, Compare economics and the investment-case ledger all read the shortfall through one accessor. A new `process_loss.outage_loss` turns an electricity outage into undelivered tonnes and EUR per process unit by its tier, net of the inventory held when the outage starts. The validator refuses a lost-production value at or below the buy-in, and warns on a `safety`-tier unit that is not on a critical bus.
