* Feat(pypsa-gui): The profile apply path now writes beyond renewable generator p_max_pu: load p_set, generator p_min_pu, marginal_cost, link efficiency and storage inflow, each with its own bounds. Profile families (renewable, load, availability, series) decide which targets they may write. Load shapes are scaled to MW, and their modelled energy is recorded and checked against the profile's. A p_min_pu above p_max_pu is refused.
