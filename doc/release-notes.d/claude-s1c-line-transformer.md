* Feat(pypsa-gui): The Expert Line and Transformer panels render from the asset schema. A line's investment is entered as a cost per MVA and km, as catalogues publish it. Its annualised capital cost and fixed O&M are derived from that times the length, and re-derived whenever the length changes: an edit, "Recalculate from coordinates", a bus moved on the map, or lengths taken from geometry. An unpriced line keeps its own lifetime. A transformer's overnight cost is entered per MVA, and its annualised capital cost is a read-only derived row.
