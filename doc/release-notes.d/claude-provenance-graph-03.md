* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant can explain why a cost has its value. The new read-only `why_this_number` tool walks the provenance graph from an asset's money field, or the Economics row's annualised cost, back to the pack price and the DC/AC ratio or Link efficiency it was scaled by. Each step comes with its value, unit and provenance stamp, and the tool says whether that input has changed since pricing (provenance-graph issue 03).
