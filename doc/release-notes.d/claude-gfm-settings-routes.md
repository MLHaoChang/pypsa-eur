* Feat(pypsa-gui): Recommended grid-forming settings can be requested for a hub project: `POST /api/campus-electrical/{name}/island/gfm-settings` runs the recommender on the last campus run's island hours as a campus job of the solve queue (progress, abort and the busy banner as the campus run), and `GET` reads the recommendation back as ready, stale (the campus run changed since) or not run. A campus rerun removes an old recommendation.
