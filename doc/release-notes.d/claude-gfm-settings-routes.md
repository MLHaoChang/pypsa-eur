* Feat(pypsa-gui): Recommended grid-forming settings can be requested for a hub project: `POST /api/campus-electrical/{name}/island/gfm-settings` runs the recommender on the last campus run's island hours as a campus job of the solve queue (progress, the busy banner, and an abort that stops the search before its next simulation), and `GET` reads the recommendation back as ready, stale (the campus run or its inputs changed since) or not run. A campus rerun removes an old recommendation; gridspine's `recommend_gfm_settings` and `gfm_settings_campus` take an optional cooperative `should_stop`.
