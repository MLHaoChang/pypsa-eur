* Feat(pypsa-gui): A results-workbook sheet whose snapshot tables would pass Excel's row limit or the workbook's cell budget is now written at a daily, weekly or monthly resolution, the same for every scenario on the sheet. MW figures and prices are hour-weighted means (prices are not load-weighted), the state of charge is the weighted mean of the state, and no bucket crosses an investment period. The key results still use every snapshot. When even monthly doesn't fit, the base keeps its tables and the other scenarios show key results only. A sheet that would still take the workbook over 25 MB is left out whole. The sheet note and About say which of these applied.
