* Feat(pypsa-gui): A generator takes HOMER-style run-hour inputs: an O&M cost per running hour, paid in the dispatch while the unit is on (it needs Unit commitment and adds to the stand-by cost), and a lifetime in running hours. The investment case replaces the generator after that many running hours at the solved hours per year, or at its calendar lifetime if sooner, and flags it. The cost breakdown lists each such generator's hours run and O&M cost. Generators without these inputs are unchanged.
