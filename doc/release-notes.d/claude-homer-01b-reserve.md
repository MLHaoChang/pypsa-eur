* Feat(pypsa-gui): Operating reserve (HOMER parity), engine and results. The solver config takes three optional shares, `reserve_share_load`, `reserve_share_pv` and `reserve_share_wind` (0–1). When any is set, the LP adds one row per snapshot: the spare capacity of dispatchable generators (a committable genset only while it is on) plus what storage units could still discharge must cover that share of the load plus those shares of the PV and wind output. So a genset can be made to run in a sunny hour, or be sized larger. The new `GET /api/results/operating_reserve` reports the reserve required and held per snapshot and the snapshots where it binds; a reserve the plant cannot hold is named in the infeasible report. Off by default; existing results and fingerprints are unchanged. The Solver Settings inputs follow.
