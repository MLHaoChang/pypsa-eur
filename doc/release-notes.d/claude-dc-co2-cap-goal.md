* Feat(pypsa-gui): A CO₂ limit (t CO₂ per year) can be set as a design goal. The solve writes it as one PyPSA CO₂ cap per investment period. The cap counts the grid at its per-period intensity, the same figure the Emissions views report. A limit no design can meet is refused with the least emissions reachable. The headline strip shows the CO₂ figure and the cost of the limit when it is reached. Expert's Emissions tab lists the goal's cap.
