* Feat(pypsa-gui): Campus island I7, frequency cuts back into sizing. When the island frequency check fails at an island hour, the campus run proposes a cut: the inertia, the upward or downward headroom, or the droop gain that hour needs at the instant of islanding. The Island section previews each cut with its primal cost estimate, the cheapest extendable unit that meets it, labelled as an estimate. "Apply cuts" adds them to the hub LP as single-hour rows, re-solves the hub and reruns the campus study. It repeats with any newly proposed cuts until nothing fails, nothing more can be cut, or three trials have run, and reports every trial. Applied cuts are kept in `island_cuts.json` beside the island sidecar, and any of them can be taken back. Bridge-energy and pickup failures get no cut.
