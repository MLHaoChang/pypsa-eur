* Fix(pypsa-gui): A bulk edit of a battery's or line's investment parts (overnight cost, lifetime, FOM share, per-km cost), or of the `max_hours`, `length` or discount rate they scale with, now re-derives `capital_cost`, `fom_cost` and `lifetime` per asset as the single edit does, and refuses a priced part without a lifetime before writing anything. The solver-input fingerprint therefore moves with the cost the solve sees, so results from before the edit no longer read as current, and a bulk FOM-share edit now reaches `fom_cost`.
