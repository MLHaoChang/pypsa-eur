* Fix(pypsa-gui): A bulk edit of a battery's or line's investment parts (overnight cost, lifetime, FOM share, per-km cost), or of the `max_hours`, `length` or discount rate they scale with, now re-derives `capital_cost`, `fom_cost` and `lifetime` per asset as the single edit does, and refuses a priced part without a lifetime before writing anything. The solve's staging re-derives `fom_cost` and `lifetime` from the parts as well as `capital_cost`, so a bulk FOM-share edit reaches the solve, and the solver-input fingerprint now also hashes the parts. A project with priced battery or line parts therefore shows its results as out of date once after upgrading; results from before a bulk part edit no longer read as current.
