* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant can cancel a background job by its handle with the new `cancel_job` tool, behind the same confirmation card as the other aborts. It reports only what it verified (`cancelled`, `cancel_requested`, `already_finished` or `finished_first`). A refused abort, a handle that no longer owns its run, or a job without a recorded owner is reported as an error with its own kind, never as a success. Stop monitoring (`POST /api/chat/{session_id}/progress/stop`) ends a chat's watching of its running solve without cancelling it: the solve finishes and its result is published, a plan step still settles from it, and another chat watching the same job keeps receiving progress.
