* Feat(pypsa-gui): A generator's no-load fuel now emits CO2. With a CO2 price, a committable fuel-curve genset pays no-load fuel × rated MW × the carrier's CO2 intensity × price in every committed hour (on its stand-by cost, for this run only), and the Results, Compare and asset emissions count it. The DC backup NPV now prices a backup genset's fuel from its fuel curve (slope plus no-load fuel, at its own fuel price), MW-weighted with the backup heat rate for gensets without one, and its cost notes say so. A fuel curve slope between 0 and 1 MWh of fuel per MWh, which means more than 100 % efficiency, gets a pre-run warning naming the generator (homer-parity 02c, 02d, 02e, 02g).
