* Feat(pypsa-gui): A fuel-curve generator's CO2 now follows its fuel curve: CO2 per MWh generated is the slope × the carrier's CO2 intensity (no longer 1 / efficiency), and the no-load fuel emits in every committed hour. With a CO2 price, both are charged in the dispatch, the no-load part on the stand-by cost for that run only. The Results, Compare and asset emissions count both. Generators without a fuel curve are unchanged. A fuel-curve generator on a CO2-emitting carrier under a CO2 cap is refused before the run, because PyPSA's cap counts CO2 from the efficiency. The DC backup NPV now prices a backup genset's fuel from its fuel curve (slope plus no-load fuel at rated output, at its own fuel price), MW-weighted with the backup heat rate for gensets without one, and its cost notes say so. A fuel curve slope between 0 and 1 MWh of fuel per MWh, which means more than 100 % efficiency, gets a pre-run warning that names the generator (homer-parity 02c, 02d, 02e, 02g).
