* Feat(pypsa-gui): The business-case engine runs the user's revenue, opex and capex lines as a second finance run on the investment case, with no counterfactual and its own hash. A P&L by year comes from the per-stream ledger, with EBITDA tied to the operating net and the cash tied to the engine's post-tax series. The KPIs are EBITDA margin and revenue per MW (each gross and net of pass-through), NPV, IRR, payback and DSCR (n/a without debt), plus a bracketed break-even on a named revenue line. Each line category gets its own stream row.
