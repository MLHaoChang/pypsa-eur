* Feat(pypsa-gui): A sampled LOLE estimate at or below the reliability goal is no longer a pass when it is not confident and one more sampled draw as heavy as the heaviest seen would push it above the goal. The coupling and margin loops keep searching on such an estimate. When nothing passes, they finish with the new status "Not confident", name the nearest plan, give the approximate number of draws that would settle it, and restore your own config. The run profiles' `eh.adequacy.lole` rule reads that status as not evaluated, never met. On the shipped EH templates, `eh_datacenter`'s coupling loop moves from met to not confident: its 1.15 h/yr estimate rests on a single sampled draw. The Monte Carlo study has no verdict against a goal, so it is unchanged.
