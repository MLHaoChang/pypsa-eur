* Feat(pypsa-gui): A sampled LOLE estimate at or below the reliability goal that is not confident, and that one more sampled shortfall hour at the heaviest snapshot would push above the goal, is no longer a pass. The coupling and margin loops keep searching on such an estimate. When nothing passes, they finish with the new status `not_confident`, name the nearest plan, give the approximate draw count that would settle it, and restore your own config. The run profiles' `eh.adequacy.lole` rule reads that status as not evaluated, never met. The Monte Carlo study has no verdict against a goal, so it is unchanged.
