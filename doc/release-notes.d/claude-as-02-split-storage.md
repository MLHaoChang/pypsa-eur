* Feat(pypsa-gui): Storage with separate power and energy (AS-02). A storage can now be a `Store` with charger and discharger links, sized as two decisions and bounded by its technology's duration range (supercapacitor 5–60 s, high-C-rate battery 0.25–1 h, energy battery 2–8 h) and an optional C-rate cap. A helper migrates a fixed-duration `StorageUnit` to this layout with the same objective. Defaults pack 2026-10-30 adds supercapacitor and high-C-rate battery costs, priced per MW and per MWh (public sources, ranges assumed).
