* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant can give a checked renewable share (`renewable-share/1`): the share of the electricity used on site that renewables supply, in percent, with the non-renewable energy and the electricity used, for the whole network or an exact window. It uses the app's renewable-fraction classification and is withheld, with a named reason, for supply paths or shed load it does not model, for a Generator with no carrier, and (the share alone) when nothing was generated.
