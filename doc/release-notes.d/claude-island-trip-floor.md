* Feat(gridspine): In the island frequency check, an event whose frequency crosses the lowest protection limit (the transition or steady-island limit, a UPS window or a load trip, whichever is lowest; and the highest on the high side) now reads as a trip at that time, not as a frequency figure the model cannot support. The event tables gain a `trip_s` column. A run written before this change shows its frequency tables as out of date in the island report until it is re-run.
