* Feat(gridspine): In the island frequency check, an event whose frequency crosses the lowest protection limit (the transition or steady-island limit, a UPS window or a load trip, whichever is lowest; and the highest on the high side) now reads as a trip at that time, not as a frequency figure the model cannot support. The event tables gain a `trip_s` column. The island report still shows the frequency tables of a run written before this change, with `trip_s` empty and a note to re-run it for trip times.
