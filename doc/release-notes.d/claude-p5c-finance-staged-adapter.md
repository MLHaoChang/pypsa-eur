* Feat(pypsa-gui): The investment case now finances staged builds read from a multi-period network: an owned asset (or campus extra) built in a later investment period becomes a later stage with its own COD (its typed date, else 1 January of its build year), which must fall in its first active period (`staged_cod_not_active_period:<asset>`); a later candidate the solve built nothing of is left out (`not_built:<asset>`), and an asset that retires inside the modelled periods is refused (`asset_retires_in_horizon:<asset>`). Unstaged cases keep their hash.
