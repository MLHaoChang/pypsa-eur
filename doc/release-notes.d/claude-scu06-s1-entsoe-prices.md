* Feat(pypsa-gui): A day-ahead price service fetches a bidding zone's historical ENTSO-E prices for one complete year with the install's ENTSO-E access token, stored and redacted like the assistant's API key. It caches each year and stores it in the Library as a price series that names its source, zone, year and fetch date. The bidding zone comes from the site pin only where a bundled lookup is sure.
