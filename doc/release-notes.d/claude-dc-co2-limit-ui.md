* Feat(pypsa-gui): The CO₂ limit goal (t CO₂/yr) is a field of the goal in Guided's Goal step and Expert's Solver settings, saved with "Save goal"; empty means no limit and 0 means no emissions. Guided and Expert Results show the year's CO₂ against the limit and the cost of the limit when it is reached, Emissions names the goal's cap "CO₂ limit", and a solve that finds no design under the limit says so with a link to raise it.
