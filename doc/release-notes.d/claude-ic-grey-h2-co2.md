* Feat(pypsa-gui): The investment case can now compare a site whose own conversion serves a non-electric load (an electrolyser feeding an H2 offtake) against buying that load instead. A `reference_purchases` entry in the commercial config (price per kg, energy per kg, CO2 per kg, source) lets the comparison buy, say, grey hydrogen as the solve saw the load, where it was refused before (`owner_conversion_load`). An optional `co2_price` (real prices by year) prices the certificate cost of that purchase per operating year and, when a grid intensity is set, of grid imports on both sides. A year before the first priced year is refused rather than extrapolated. The avoided CO2 cost is shown as its own figure beside the NPV and in the workbook, and both new caveats appear beside the headline figures. Cases without these inputs are unchanged.
