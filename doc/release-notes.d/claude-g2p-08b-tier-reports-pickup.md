* Feat(pypsa-gui): Grid-to-process criticality tiers reach the adequacy reports. Each link outage in the FMEA class-B sweep now reports, per investment period, the process units it cuts off and their lost tonnes and EUR per year, priced at the lost-production value. A unit is cut off when its electricity supply or one of its feedstocks has no source left among the components active in that period (an electrolyser outage stops the Haber-Bosch loop it feeds; a feedstock tank is not credited, flagged), and a process unit's own outage stops it. The event is averaged over every start hour, the occurrence uses the year's own hours, and an outage longer than the period is capped at one cycle, flagged, and marked a lower bound. The DtC stress rows report their re-solve's own lost production per product, and the sequential Monte Carlo says the figure is not established (it samples no link outages). Without explicit `pickup_blocks`, an island process unit's draw is now a largest-load-step (E2) check, and the island study reports the pickup sequence by criticality tier; a process unit with no tier is picked up as process-critical and flagged. No shipped template has a process unit on an island, so no template's E2 step changes.
