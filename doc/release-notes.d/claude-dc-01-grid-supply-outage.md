* Feat(pypsa-gui): The data-centre templates (`eh_datacenter`, `eh_datacenter_staged`) give the upstream grid supply generic, public outage data, a forced-outage rate of 7.506e-06 and an MTTR of 0.487 h. They come from the TSO-level SAIDI and SAIFI (median year, 2011-2018) in the CEER-ECRB 7th Benchmarking Report on the Quality of Electricity and Gas Supply (2022). The reserve margin and the multi-objective front can now price the grid supply instead of refusing it. Both values are ledger rows marked "assumed" in the Connection section, to confirm or replace. Projects created before this change keep their data and are not migrated: their grid supply still has no outage data, and the margin still refuses it by name.

* Feat(pypsa-gui): The reserve margin no longer credits generation behind a point of connection above what the connection carries. Generators and storage that reach the site only through a `grid_import` Link are credited as one group per investment period, at most what the Link delivers (its rating times its lowest `p_max_pu` in the period, within its energization schedule, times its efficiency) times its own availability. A chain of imports counts at its tightest Link. Which supply is behind the import is worked out per period from the branches active then, in the direction each Link can carry power, and the site is every bus with a Load (or flagged critical). Each such asset's facts and its row in the margin table show its share of the capped credit. An extendable candidate behind the import (`reserve_margin_import_group_extendable`) or an extendable `grid_import` Link (`reserve_margin_import_link_extendable`) is refused by name. Networks without a `grid_import` Link are unchanged. On the data-centre templates the grid supply now counts for the 40 MW at 0.995 efficiency (single-year) or 15/55/55 MW (staged) connection, not its 500 MW.
