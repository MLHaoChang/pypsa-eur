* Feat(pypsa-gui): The data-centre templates (`eh_datacenter`, `eh_datacenter_staged`) give the upstream grid supply generic, public outage data, a forced-outage rate of 7.506e-06 and an MTTR of 0.487 h. They come from the TSO-level SAIDI and SAIFI (median year, 2011-2018) in the CEER-ECRB 7th Benchmarking Report on the Quality of Electricity and Gas Supply (2022). The reserve margin and the multi-objective front can now price the grid supply instead of refusing it. Both values are ledger rows marked "assumed" in the Connection section, to confirm or replace. Projects created before this change keep their data and are not migrated: their grid supply still has no outage data, and the margin still refuses it by name.

* Feat(pypsa-gui): The reserve margin no longer credits generation behind a point of connection above what the connection carries. Generators and storage that reach the site only through a `grid_import` Link are credited as one group per investment period, at most the Link's energized MW times its own availability; each such asset's facts say so. An extendable candidate behind the import is refused by name (`reserve_margin_import_group_extendable`). Networks without a `grid_import` Link are unchanged. On the data-centre templates the grid supply now counts for the 40 MW (single-year) or 15/55/55 MW (staged) connection, not its 500 MW.
