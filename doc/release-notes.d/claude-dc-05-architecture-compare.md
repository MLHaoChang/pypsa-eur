* Feat(pypsa-gui): Data-centre projects take a power architecture (AC with an LV UPS, MV UPS, 800 VDC or a solid-state transformer) in the `dc_load` create body. A new Power architecture section on the Grid study tab compares all four against the project's own on the saved network, with no solve and nothing written. It reports the efficiency to the rack, the effective PUE, facility and loss energy, the peak within the import limit, capex, and the NPV against this project at the central values and at the range ends ("not distinguishable" when the interval spans zero). Without a readable tariff energy rate the compare asks for a price per MWh, which Guided then offers in place. It also shows the grid services each architecture can offer, with the scorecard's frequency-support and voltage-control verdicts; an architecture-only service is assumed and never passes alone. The conversion-loss chains and capex rows are assumed, with ranges and sources, in the new defaults pack version 2026-11-07 (2026-11-06 is unchanged). An unset architecture reads as AC and changes no network, ledger or scorecard byte.
