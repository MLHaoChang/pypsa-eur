* Feat(pypsa-gui): The results workbook's Scenarios sheet and view sheets now also show your own experiment cases on the open project, after its sensitivity runs and up to eight runs in all. A case's figures are shown only when its saved network still has the input fingerprint of its receipt and its run evidence is unchanged. Otherwise, or when it failed, has not completed, or was solved on a different base, horizon, mode or solver settings, the case shows the reason and no figures. An experiment case is no longer also listed as a sensitivity run. Cases you cannot open, other users' cases and cases over the cap are counted in the sheet notes and in About, and never named.
