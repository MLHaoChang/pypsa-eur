* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant compares a base with up to 8 of its sensitivity cases in one read (`compare_sensitivities`): per metric, each case's value, signed delta and delta % against the base, computed by the Compare view's own engines and shown as one table cell per case under the metric's label and unit, money in M€, k€ or € so each row reads. A case that cannot be compared (unsolved, out of date, changed since branching, or on another horizon, mode or solver settings) is listed with its reason and no deltas. `rank_by` returns a qualified preference with the case's declared changes beside its delta, or says there is no defensible preference. On the user's yes, Compare opens for base vs that case on the matching tab, scrolled into view, with "Back" to where they were. The same table exports as JSON (`export_sensitivity_comparison`). `compare_scenarios` can now focus the Line loading tab.

* Fix(pypsa-gui): A queued solve of a project with an uploaded profile now records its solve inputs with that project's own uploaded series, so its results read as current afterwards instead of "out of date" forever; editing the profile still marks them out of date. Projects solved before this release keep their wrong fingerprint until their next solve.
