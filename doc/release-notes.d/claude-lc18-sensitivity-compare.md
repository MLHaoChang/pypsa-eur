* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant compares a base with up to 8 of its sensitivity cases in one read (`compare_sensitivities`): per metric, each case's value, signed delta and delta % against the base, computed by the Compare view's own engines and shown with its labels and units. A case that cannot be compared (unsolved, out of date, changed since branching, or on another horizon, mode or solver settings) is listed with its reason and no deltas. `rank_by` returns a qualified preference with the case's declared changes beside its delta, or says there is no defensible preference. On the user's yes, Compare opens for base vs that case on the matching tab, with "Back" to where they were. The same table exports as JSON (`export_sensitivity_comparison`). `compare_scenarios` can now focus the Line loading tab.
