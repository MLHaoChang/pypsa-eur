* Feat(pypsa-gui): The single-year data center, hydrogen hub, island microgrid and renewables IPP examples can be created for any planning years. `POST /api/projects/from_template/eh_datacenter`, `/eh_h2_hub`, `/eh_microgrid` and `/eh_ipp_hybrid` take `{"planning": {"start_year", "end_year"}}`. The start year dates the modelled week (the IPP's hourly year, with 29 February dropped in a leap year); the end year is the horizon. The microgrid and the IPP run their investment case from the start year to the end year, so they need an end year after the start year: the microgrid closes one year before the start, the IPP two. The IPP's 2030 illustrative prices, profiles, tariff and PPA prices and its observed 2024 prices are re-dated by hour of year and taken as start-year money with no conversion; the finance money year and every price series' money year move together. The ledger records the money year and the re-dating, and the create mints and pins the price series for the entered year. Without `planning`, all four templates are unchanged (E1d).
