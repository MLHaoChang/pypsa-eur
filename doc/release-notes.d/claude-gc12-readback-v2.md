* Feat(gridspine): The pack read-back accepts battery contract version 2 and judges "stays connected" on every judged case kind from the result's `connected` channel (PoC breaker status). The first 0 after the event is the trip and fails the case at that time; for fault (LVRT/HVRT) cases a trip only fails while the voltage is within the ride-through curve, as before. Blank status samples with no 0 read unknown ("status poll failed at t=…"), and the case passes only when every judged sample reads 1. A version 1 pack has no such channel, so its "stays connected" row reads unknown ("no connected channel (contract v1)") on every kind and no case passes. A `connected` channel in a unit other than `status`, or with a sample other than 0, 1 or blank, leaves only that case unknown, naming the unit or the sample.
