* Feat(pypsa-gui): A campus run's EMT pack can take its case results back (`POST /api/campus-electrical/{name}/psse/{bundle}/emt/results`, re-judge with `.../emt/judge`). The pack is judged against the run's grid-code profile by the pack read-back, and the stored judgment is served as written, with its status counts, at `GET /api/campus-electrical/{name}/judgment`. The grid-compliance tracker reads it as the judged dynamic results of its deliverables.
