* Feat(pypsa-gui): The grid-readiness scorecard takes your own connection ramp and oscillation limits, and where the fault level comes from (code, datasheet, measured or assumed). They are kept in a bundled `grid_readiness_limits.json` that travels with snapshots and bundles, and the template file is never rewritten. `PUT`/`DELETE /api/campus-electrical/{name}/grid-readiness/limits/{item}` sets and resets them; a confirmed limit clears its to-check item in the project flow.
