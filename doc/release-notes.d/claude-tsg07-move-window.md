* Feat(pypsa-gui): A maintenance availability series can move one planned outage window by whole days (`move_window`, for example "move the maintenance window two weeks later"). The series is re-generated from the moved window. The edits before the move keep their own hours. Moving a window and moving it back gives the original series bit for bit. The op is recorded like other refinements, so it is replayed on re-generate. The copilot proposes it behind the old→new card, and the editor's refinement list offers it on maintenance series.
