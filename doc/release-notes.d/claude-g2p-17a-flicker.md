* Feat(gridspine, pypsa-gui): Flicker screen at the point of connection for loads that step their power, such as burst-fired heaters (grid-to-process issue 17, flicker part). `gridspine/static/flicker.py` turns each unit's largest power step and its rate into the relative voltage change d = step / S_k and the short-term flicker severity Pst, using the IEC 61000-3-7 analytic method with rectangular steps. Units are summed at the PoC with the general summation law (alpha 3, assumed), and Plt is taken equal to Pst for a pattern that repeats steadily. A unit outside the analytic method's range (changes under 1 s apart, or d above 10 %) is reported as unknown and needs a flicker-meter study; it never passes. A process unit gains `flicker_step_mva` and `flicker_changes_per_min`, where switching on and switching off each count as a change. A step or rate of 0 declares a steady load, and a unit with either input missing is reported as "no input", never skipped. The campus study writes `campus_flicker.csv`. Grid-code profiles gain `pst_limit` and `plt_limit`; the EU and generic profiles carry the indicative IEC TR 61000-3-7 HV-EHV planning levels Pst 0.8 and Plt 0.6, tagged assumed. The grid-readiness scorecard gains a flicker row after the harmonic row. It reads the PoC short-circuit level and judges on both limits; it is a screening estimate, and above the limit a flicker-meter study is needed.
