* Feat(pypsa-gui): Heat and steam as modelled products (grid-to-process issue 15). A heat bus states its supply temperature and a steam bus its header pressure, in bar(g); a device states its reach (max supply temperature, max steam pressure). The validator refuses a device that feeds a level above its reach, and a level with demand but no supplier. A new `steam` carrier, a band-wise heat demand input, a hybrid gas plus electric supply builder and a two-level demo are added; band edges and demo values are labelled `demo`. A network without levels behaves as before.
