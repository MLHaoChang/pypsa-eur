* Feat(pypsa-gui): A decision study can compare its base project's supply architectures (HOMER-style categorized results): one solve per combination of PV, wind, battery, genset and grid supply, with the other assets omitted, tabulating each architecture's best system with its annual cost, capex and renewable fraction. An infeasible architecture is listed, not dropped. Served by `POST/GET /api/projects/{name}/studies/{study_id}/supply-architectures` and the `compare_supply_architectures` / `get_supply_architectures` chat tools.
