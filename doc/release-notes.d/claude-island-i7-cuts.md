* Feat(gridspine): Frequency cuts for the island sizing loop (plan I7). `frequency.propose_cuts` and `campus_study.cuts_campus` turn each failing frequency event into the inertia, headroom or droop gain its hour needs, in `campus_freq_cuts.csv`, for the user to apply in the hub. Each cut is found on the I6 engine with the units' response held at the current solution, and is checked against the closed-form nadir. Each event is re-run at its own table's step and horizon, until it settles; a failure the re-run does not reproduce is listed as infeasible. When a grid-forming unit saturates, the droop and inertia cuts come from the event re-run with its headroom. A unit-trip (E3) failure is listed as needing N−1 cover, not as a cut. The droop cut counts no unit at its limit, no cut above 100 times the current value is offered, and an infeasible row never hides a feasible one.
