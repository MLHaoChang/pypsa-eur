* Feat(pypsa-gui): Projects now have a scope that sets what the Run computes: six work cards (Capacity, AC power flow & grid, Control, Reliability, Commercial & finance, Hand-off), each with up to three sub-options. The scope is stored in `flow_scope.json`, which travels with bundles, and is served by `GET/PUT /api/projects/{name}/flow/scope`. Every example opens with its own card preset. Sub-options the engine can't run yet read "Not yet available". Deselecting a card hides its blocks, and its items stop counting toward or blocking the flow (`in_scope: false`). Deselecting Reliability also leaves the study out of the Run. The scope component sits in Guided's Goal card and in Expert's Solver settings.
