* Feat(pypsa-gui): Projects now have a scope that sets what the Run computes: six work cards (Capacity, AC power flow & grid, Control, Reliability, Commercial & finance, Hand-off), each with up to three sub-options. The scope is stored in `flow_scope.json`, which travels with bundles, and is served by `GET/PUT /api/projects/{name}/flow/scope`. Every example opens with its own card preset. Sub-options the engine can't run yet read "Not yet available". Load flow and N-1 security show their solver setting, read-only. Deselecting a card hides its blocks, and its items stop counting toward or blocking the flow (`in_scope: false`). Deselecting Reliability also leaves the study out of the Run. The scope component sits in Guided's Goal card, as a one-line summary with "Change", and in Expert's Solver settings.

* Feat(pypsa-gui): Behaviour change for the IPP hybrid template (`eh_ipp_hybrid`): by default its scope leaves Reliability off, so its Run no longer includes the reliability study. Switching Reliability on brings the study back. An explicit study start (the study route or the chat tool) still works whatever the scope.
