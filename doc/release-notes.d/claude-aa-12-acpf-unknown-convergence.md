* Fix(pypsa-gui): AC power flow no longer records snapshots as converged when PyPSA reports no convergence for them. This covers a missing or unreadable result, a NaN or non-boolean cell, and a snapshot the result does not cover. Such snapshots are now unknown: the load-flow page shows "k / n · u unknown" or "Unknown / n" and a neutral "?" badge, and the run is refused as "not known to have converged" until it is re-run.
