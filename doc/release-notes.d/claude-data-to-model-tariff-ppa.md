* Feat(pypsa-gui): The assistant can write values from an uploaded PDF onto the project's import tariff and PPA contracts (data-to-model slice 2). Each rate or price is cited by its page and the exact words that hold it and goes onto an item or contract that already exists; the unit must be the item's own (a price per MWh into a per-kWh rate is the one conversion), the currency must be stated and match the project's, a PPA price's money year is recorded as the contract's base year and never adjusted, and a tariff or contract pinned to the Commercial library is left to the library. The write checks that the commercial settings have not changed since the card was shown and runs the commercial preflight first.
