## What it means

This lists the voltage dips the unit rides through, as pairs of dip depth in percent and duration in seconds, written depth:duration, such as 20:0.5, 40:0.15, 100:0.02. The unit rides a dip when some pair is at least as deep and as long. Leave it empty when unknown.

## Example

A drive that rides a 40 % dip lasting 0.15 s.
