## What it means

Tick this to let the optimiser decide how big the line should be, anywhere between the smallest and largest size you set. For every MVA it adds, the annualised cost is charged to the plan. Leave it unticked to treat the size as fixed. Use it when you are asking how much to build rather than testing a design you already have.

## Example

Tick it on a feeder to see whether it needs reinforcing beyond its current 10 MVA.
