## What it means

This is the flow on port 4 for each unit drawn from the input side. A positive value means the port bus receives it, such as 0.4 MWh of heat per MWh of fuel from a CHP. A negative value means the link draws it from the port bus, such as a feed of 4.6 per unit of input. It is only read when a bus is set on port 4.

## Example

A CHP with 0.4 on port 4 sends 0.4 MWh of heat out for each MWh of fuel drawn.
