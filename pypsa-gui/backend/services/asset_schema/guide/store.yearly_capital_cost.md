## What it means

This is a calculated value: the upfront cost turned into a yearly cost over the lifetime at the discount rate, with fixed O&M charged on top. The optimiser pays it for every MWh it adds, for the share of a year your snapshots represent. You do not type it; change the upfront cost, lifetime or discount rate instead.

## Example

A 20 EUR/kWh tank over 25 years at 7 % comes to roughly 1.7 EUR/kWh/yr.
