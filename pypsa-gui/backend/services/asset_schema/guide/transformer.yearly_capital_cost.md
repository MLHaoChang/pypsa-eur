## What it means

This is a calculated value: the upfront cost turned into a yearly cost over the lifetime at the discount rate, with fixed O&M charged on top. The optimiser pays it for every MVA it adds, for the share of a year your snapshots represent. You do not type it; change the upfront cost, lifetime or discount rate instead.

## Example

A 40000 EUR/MVA transformer over 40 years at 7 % comes to roughly 3000 EUR/MVA/yr.
