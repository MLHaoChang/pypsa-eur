## What it means

This is a calculated value: the upfront cost turned into a yearly cost over the lifetime at the discount rate, with fixed O&M charged on top. The optimiser pays it for every MW it adds, for the share of a year your snapshots represent. You do not type it; change the upfront cost, lifetime or discount rate instead.

## Example

A 700 EUR/kW field over 25 years at 7 % comes to roughly 60 EUR/kW/yr before O&M.
